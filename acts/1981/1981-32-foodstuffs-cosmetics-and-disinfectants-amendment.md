ACT

To amend the Foodstuffs, Cosmetics and Disinfectants Act, 1972, so as to substitute certain designations therein; entrust the powers, duties and functions of inspectors in respect of foodstuffs also to certain other persons; regulate afresh the inspection and control of imported foodstuffs, cosmetics and disinfectants under certain circumstances and the disposal of prohibited articles which have been imported; and make further provision for the prescribing of standards for different attributes of foodstuffs, (,:Osmetics or,disinfectants; and to provide for incidental matters.
