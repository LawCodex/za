# Special Appropriation Act 50 of 2024

ACT

To appropriate an additional amount of money for the requirements of the State for the 2024/25 financial year; and to provide for matters connected therewith. B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Appropriation of additional amount of money for requirements of State

1\. (1) Appropriations by Parliament of additional money from the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State in the 2024/25 financial year and the main division within the vote, and for the purposes that are specified, are set out in the

## Schedule.

(2) The provisions of the Appropriation Act, 2024 (Act No. 40 of 2024), apply to appropriations envisaged in subsection (1) with the necessary changes.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Special Appropriation Act, 2024.
