# Skills Development Levies Amendment Act 24 of 2010

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(Enlilish text signed by the President.) (Assented to 3 December 2010.)

ACT

To amend the Skills Development Levies Act, 1999, so as to amend the definitions of "Director-General" and "Minister"; and to provide for matters connected therewith, B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows.- Amendment of section 1 of Act 9 of 1999 1, SecIion I of the Skills Development Levies Act, 1999, is hereby amended by-

(a) the substitution for the definition of "Director-General" of the following definition:

" 'Director-General' means Ihe Director-General of [Labour) Higher Education and Training;"; and (b) the substitution for the definition of "Minister" of the following definition; .. 'Minister' means the Minister of [Labour] Higher Education and Training;".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is the Skills Development Levies Amendment Act, 2010.
