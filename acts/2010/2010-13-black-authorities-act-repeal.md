# Black Authorities Act Repeal Act 13 of 2010

ACT

To repeal the Black Authorities Act, 1951; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 1. B EIT ENACTED by thc Parliament of the Repohlic of South Africa, as follows:- Repeal of Act 68 of 1951

1\. The Black Authorities Act, 1951, is hereby repealed on-

(a) 31 December 2010; or (b) the date on which the last of the provinces of KwaZulu-Natal and Limpopo has repealed those provisions that were assigned to them, whichever occurs first.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Black Authorilies Act Repeal Act, 2010. J()
