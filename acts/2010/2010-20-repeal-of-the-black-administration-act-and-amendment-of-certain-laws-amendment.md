# Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Amendment Act 20 of 2010

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to I December 20/0.)

ACT

To amend the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act. 2005, so as to substitute a date; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

PARLIAMENT of the Republic of South Africa enacts, as follows:- Amendment of section l of Act 28 of 2005, as amended by section 1 of Act 8 of 2006, section 1 of Act 13 of 2007, section 1 of Act 7 of 2008 and section l of Act 20 of 2009

1\. Section 1(3) of the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act, No. 28 of 2005, is hereby amended by the substitution in subsection (3) for paragraph (a) of the following paragraph:

"(a) (JO December 2010] 30 December 2012; or".

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act is called the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of l 0 Certain Laws Amendmenl Act, 20LO, and comes into operation on 29 December 20LO.
