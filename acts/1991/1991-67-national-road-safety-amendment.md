# National Road Safety Amendment Act 67 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the National Road Safety Act, 1972, so as to make different provision with regard to the approval of the annual estimates of the income and expenditure of the National Road Safety Council which shall be submitted once every financial year by that Council to the Minister, and with regard to the approval of supplementary estimates of expenditure which may be submitted by the Council to the Minister during the course of a fmancial year; and to make different provision with regard to the amounts in which and the times at which certain money shall be paid to the Council; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 14 May 1991.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 15 of Act 9 of 1972, as amended by section 6 of Act 59 of 1981

1\. Section 15 of the National Road Safety Act, 1972, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection ( 4) of the following subsection:

"( 4) Once during every financial year (which shall end on the thirty-first of March), at such time as the Minister may direct, the council shall submit to the Minister for his approval, granted with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance and of the Minister of Mineral and Energy Affairs and Public Enterprises, estimates of the income and expenditure of the council during the ensuing financial year, and the Council may also, during the course of a financial year, submit to the Minister, for his approval, granted with such concurrence, supplementary estimates of expenditure for that year.". #### 3. Short title and commencement

3\. This Act shall be called the National Road Safety Amendment Act, 1991, and shall come into operation on 1 April 1991.
