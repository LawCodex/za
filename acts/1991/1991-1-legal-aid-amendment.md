# Legal Aid Amendment Act 1 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Legal Aid Act, 1969, so as to alter the constitution of the Legal Aid Board; and to exclude the territory of South-West Africa from the application of the Act; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 18 March 1991 .) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

2\. Section 9A of the Legal Aid Act, 1969, is hereby repealed.
