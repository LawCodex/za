# Appropriation Act 132 of 1991

ACT

To appropriate amounts of money for the requirements of the State during the financial year ending 31 March 1992 •.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 27 June 1991.) B EIT ENACfE_D by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Afnca, as follows:- - - ·

#### 1. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of State, excluding provinces

1\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the State Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State, excluding the provinces, during the financial year ending 31 March 1992, as a charge to the State Revenue Account, the amounts of money shown in column 1 of Schedule 1.

#### 2. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of province of the Cape of GoodHope

2\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the State Revenue Fund for the requirements of the province of the Cape of Good Hope during the financial year ending 31 March 1992, as a charge to the Account for Provincial Services : Cape, the amounts of money shown in column 1 of S~hedule 2. · 15

#### 3. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of province of Natal

3\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), . there are hereby appropriated out of the State Revenue Fund for the requirements of the province of Natal during the financial year ending 31 March 1992, as a charge to the Account for Provincial Services : Natal, the amounts of ' money shown in column 1 of Schedule 3.

#### 4. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of province of the Orange Free State ·

4\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the State Revenue Fund for the requirements of the province of the Orange Free State during the financial year ending 31 March 1992, as a charge to the Account for Provincial Services : Orange Free State, the amounts of money shown in column 1 of Schedule 4.

#### 5. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of province of the Transvaal

5\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the State Revenue Fund for the requirements of the province of the Transvaal during the financial year ending 31 March 1992, as a charge to the Account for Provincial Services : Transvaal, the amounts of money shown in column 1 of Schedule 5.

#### 6. Short title

6\. This Act shall be called the Appropriation Act, 1991.
