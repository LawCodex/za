# Finance Act (House of Assembly) 44 of 1991

ACT

To make provision for the remission of certain money owing by the Groot Constantia Control Board.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) {Assented to 8 April 1991 .) EIT ENACTED by the State President and the House of Assembly of the B Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Remission of certain money owing by Groot Constantia Control Board

1\. The Groot Constantia Control Board, established by section 2 of the Groot Constantia State Estate Control Act, 1975 (Act No. 24 of 1975), is hereby discharged from all liability in respect of an amount of R5 531 970 which was granted to it as an interest-free loan out of the Revenue Account: House of Assembly, referred to in section 2 (1) (b) (i) of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975).

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Finance Act (House of Assembly), 1991. 10
