# Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Amendment Act 19 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments .

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Act, 1966, so as to further regulate the abolition of the committees of management; and to regulate the ownership of those portions of the common grazing ground of the Weltevreden Agricultural Settlement which were not divided into lots; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 15 March 1991.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows: -

#### 1. Amendment of section 3 of Act 53 of 1966

1\. Section 3 of the Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Act, 1966 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), is hereby amended- ( a) by the substitution for paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of the following paragraph:

"(a) [provision for the management of] irrigation boards have been estab lished in terms of section 79 of the Water Act, 1956 (Act No. 54 of 1956), for the areas referred to in the definition of "Settlement" in section 1 of the [said Ordinance has been made in terms of the Small-Holdings Ordinance, 1954 (Ordinance No. 17 of 1954) of the Orange Free State] Agricultural Settlements Private Ordinance, 1907, of the Orange Free State; and"; and (b) by the substitution for subsection (2) of the following subsection:

"(2) Subject to the provisions of section 2, as from the date specified in any proclamation issued under subsection (1) of this section, all the assets and rights of the Committees of Management shall vest in, and all their liabilities and obligations shall devolve upon, [any peri-urban authority exercising control in terms of the said Small-Holdings Ordinance, 1954, over the areas] the irrigation board concerned which has been established for an area mentioned in subsection (1) (a) of this section.".

#### 2. Amendment of section 3A of Act 53 of 1966, as inserted by section 1 of Act 38 of 1990

2\. Section 3A of the principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (1) of the following subsection:

"(1) The ownership of those portions of the common grazing ground of the Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural [Settlement] Settlements which have not been divided into lots, shall vest in the State.".

#### 3. Repeal of section 4 of Act 53 of 1966

3\. Section 4 of the principal Act is hereby repealed.

#### 4. Substitution of section 5 of Act 53 of 1966, as substituted by section 2 of Act 38 of 1990

4\. The following section is hereby substituted for section 5 of the principal Act:

"Compensation in respect of rights vesting in State

5\. No compensation shall be payable in respect of any rights vesting in the State by virtue of the provisions of section 2 or 3A [or 4]. ".

#### 5. Short title and commencement

5\. This Act shall be called the Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Amendment Act, 1991, and shall come into operation on a date fixed by the State President by proclamation in the Gazette.
