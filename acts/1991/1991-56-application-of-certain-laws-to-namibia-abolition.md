# Application of Certain Laws to Namibia Abolition Act 56 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

1 ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

ACT

To amend certain laws so as to abolish the application thereof in respect of Namibia; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 15 May 1991 .) EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of B South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Abolition of application of certain laws to Namibia

1\. The laws mentioned in the Schedule are hereby amended to the extent set out in the third column of the Schedule. 5

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Application of Certain Laws to Namibia Abolition Act, 1991.
