# National Momuments Amendment Act 25 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the National Monuments Act, 1969, so as to authorize the council to pay to certain members of the council, of its committees or of the committees established under section 3A the remuneration determined by the Minister with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance; to authorize the council to pay to members of the council or of such committees allowances so determined; and to grant to the council greater autonomy with regard to the determination of its establishment, and the payment of remuneration, allowances, subsidies and other benefits to its officers and employees; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 18 March 1991.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Substitution of section 6 of Act 28 of 1969, as amended by section 6 of Act 13 of 1981

1\. The following section is hereby substituted for section 6 of the National Monuments Act, 1969 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act):

"Remuneration and allowances of members of council and committees

6\. The council may pay to a member of the council or any committee thereof or any committee established by section 3A-

(a) who is not in the full-time employment of the State or the council, the remuneration; and (b) the allowances, determined by the Minister with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance.".

#### 2. Amendment of section 8 of Act 28 of 1969, as amended by section 6 of Act 35 of 1979

2\. Section 8 of the principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of the following paragraph:

"(a) (i) The council may appoint such officers and employees as it may deem necessary for the performance of its functions and the carrying out o its duties, and shall determine their remuneration, allowances, subsi dies and other benefits in accordance with a system approved by the Minister with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance, as sue system is amended from time to time.

(ii) Any person who immediately prior to the commencement of th National Monuments Amendment Act, 1991, was an officer or em ployee in the employment of the council shall, subject to the provisions Act No. 25, 1991 NATIONAL MONUMENTS AMENDMENT ACT, 1991 of subparagraph (iii), be deemed to have been appointed as an officer or employee, as the case may be, of the council, as from such commencement.

(iii) The remuneration, allowances, subsidies and other benefits of any person mentioned in subparagraph (ii) shall not be less favourable than 5 . the remuneration, allowances, subsidies and other benefits to which such person was entitled immediately before the commencement of the said Amendment Act.".

#### 3. Short title

3\. This Act shall be called the National Monuments Amendment Act, 1991. 10
