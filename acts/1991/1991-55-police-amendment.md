# Police Amendment Act 55 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

I ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to place a prohibition on members of the South African Police, excluding members of the Police Reserve, with regard to their membership of or participation or involvement in political parties, other institutions with political objects and their activities and institutions, councils or bodies referred to in section 84(l)(j) of the Provincial Government Act, 1961; and to prohibit members of the Police Reserve while serving in the Force from taking part in the activities of political parties and other institutions with political objects; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 2. Insertion of section 35 in Act 7 of 1958

2\. The following section is hereby inserted in the principal Act after section 34G:

“Prohibition on political activities of members of the Force

35\. (1) (a) No member of the Force, including a member of the Act No. 55,1991 POLICE AMENDMENT ACT, 1991 Reserve Police Force whether or not while serving in the Force, but excluding a member referred to in paragraph (b), may be a member of or take part in the activities of a political party (whether or not it is registered) or any other movement, organization, body or association with political objects, or stand for election as a member of, or serve on, an institution, council or body referred to in section 84(l)(f) of the Provincial Government Act, 1961 (Act No. 32 of 1961) (in this section referred to as a local authority).

(b) No member of the Police Reserve may, while serving in the Force, take part in the activities of such political party or any other movement, organization, body or association with political objects.

(2) The provisions of subsection (1) shall not be construed as to prohibit a member of the Force-

(a) to attend a public meeting of a political party or a movement, organization, body or association with political objects;

(b) to cast a vote in respect of the election of a person to a body established under the provisions of any law, by way of any form of election;

(c) to exercise powers or perform duties as police officer under the 1 provisions of any other law.”. 20

#### 3. Short title and commencement

3\. This Act shall be called the Police Amendment Act, 1991, and shall come into operation on a date fixed by the State President by proclamation in the Gazette.
