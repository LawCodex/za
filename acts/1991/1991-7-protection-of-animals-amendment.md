# Protection of Animals Amendment Act 7 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Performing Animals Protection Act, 1935, so as to extend the provisions of the Act to include dogs used for safeguarding; to review the fines that may be imposed in respect of offences; and to exclude the territory of South West Africa from the application of the Act; to amend the Animals Protection Act, 1962, so as to further define and extend actions constituting offences; to review the fines that may be imposed in respect of offences; and to exclude the territory of South West Africa from the application of the Act; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 15 March 1991.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Substitution of section 1 of Act 24 of 1935

1\. The following section is hereby substituted for section 1 of the Performing Animals Protection Act, 1935 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act) :

"Restriction on exhibition and training of performing animals and use of dogs for safeguarding

1\. No person shall exhibit or train or cause or permit to be exhibited or trained for exhibition any animal of which he is the owner or has the lawful custody or use any dog for safeguarding unless such person is the holder of a licence.".

#### 8. Substitution of section 9 of Act 24 of 1935

8\. The following section is hereby substituted for section 9 of the principal Act:

"Saving

9\. Nothing in this Act shall apply to the confinement or training of animals for military, police or sporting purposes or the purpose of an agricultural show, horse show, dog show, caged bird show or any public zoological gardens, or to the exhibition of animals at a military or police tournament or a gymkhana, or at any such show or in any such gardens or to the use of a dog for safeguarding by the South African Defence Force, the South African Police or the Prisons Service.".

#### 17. Short title and commencement

17\. (1) This Act shall be called the Protection of Animals Amendment Act, 1991, and shall come into operation on a date fixed by the State President by proclamation in the Gazette.
