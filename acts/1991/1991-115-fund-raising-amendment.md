# Fund-raising Amendment Act 115 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

1 ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Fund-raising Act, 1978, so as to establish a fund for the temporary rendering of aid to members of communities being victims of violence; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 27 June 1991.) EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic B of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Substitution of heading to Chapter II of Act 107 of 1978

1\. The following heading is hereby substituted for the heading to Chapter I1 of the Fund-raising Act, 1978 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act):

“DISASTER RELIEF FUND, SOUTH AFRICAN DEFENCE DENT’S FUND AND SOCIAL RELIEF FUND”.
