# Environment Conservation Amendment Act 98 of 1991

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Environment Conservation Act, 1989, so as to extend the definition of' 'local authority"; and to provide that regulations which may affect the activities of any local authority or government institution shall only be made applicable within the area of jurisdiction of such local authority or government institution with its concurrence; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
