# Fertilizers, Farm Feeds and Remedies Amendment Act 60 of 1970

ACT

To amend the Fertilizers, Farm Feeds and Remedies Act, 1947, relating to definitions, registration, fertilizers, farm feeds, remedies, inspectors, analysts, offences, regulations and returns.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 21st September, 1970.)

"'stock remedy' means a substance intended or offered to be used in connection with domestic animals, livestock, poultry or wild animals (including wild birds), for the prevention, treatment or cure of any disease, infection or other unhealthy condi tion, or for the maintenance or improvement of health, growth, production or working capacity, or for the lasting capacity of carcasses, but excluding any preparation dispensed on the pre scription of a veterinarian for a particular patient or group of patients;".
