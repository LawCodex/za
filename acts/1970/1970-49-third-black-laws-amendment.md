# Third Black Laws Amendment Act 49 of 1970

ACT

To amend section 4 of the Bantu Trust and Land Act, 1936, so as to regulate further the fnnctions of the Bantu Affairs Com mission and its members in relation to the affairs of the South African Bantu Trust; to amend the Promotion of Bantu Self-government Act, 1959, so as to regulate further the appointment of representatives of Bantu in urban areas and to define further their powers, fnnctions and duties;

to amend the Bantu Affairs Act, 1959, so as to alter the constitution of the Bantu Affairs Commission; to extend the power to make regulations in regard to the affairs of the said commission; to apply the provisions of the last-mentioned Act in connection with the said commission in respect of South-West Mrica; and to substitute the words "State President" for the word "Governor-General"; to amend the Urban Bantu Conncils Act, 1961, so as to alter the constitu tion of urban Bantu councils; to amend the Transkei Constitu tion Act, 1963, so as to empower the Legislative Assembly in the Transkei to make laws in relation to customary unions; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to lith September, 1970.)
