# National War Fund Act 81 of 1970

ACT

To provide for the amalgamation of the funds known as the Governor-General's Fund (Great War 1914-1918) and the National War Fund (1939-1945); and for Incidental matters.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 30th September, 1970.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Act No. 81, 1970 · 1. (I) The fund known as the Governor-General's Fund Amalgamation of (Great War 1914-1918) and the fund known as the National war funds. (a) any reference in the said trust deed and constitution, and in so far as it may be necessary or expedient in order to give effect to the provisions of this Act, in any related document, to the National War Fund (1939-1945), shall be construed as a reference to the National War Fund;
