# National Monuments Amendment Act 22 of 1970

ACT

To amend the National Monuments Act, 1969, in order to provide for the expropriation of certain land.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 3rd March, 1970.)

#### 1. BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Act No. 22, 1970

1\. The following section is hereby inserted in the National Inser!ion of section Monuments Act, 1969, after section 14: ~~~~n Act 28 of ·:Expropria- 14A. (1) Notwithstanding anything to the contJon of land. trary in any law contained, the Minister of Agriculture may, at the request of the council, and in consultation with the Minister, expropriate for the benefit of the council any land declared or about to be declared under this Act to be a national monu ment, provided the council has satisfied the Minister that it is unable to acquire such land on reasonable terms.

(2) The provisions of the Expropriation Act, 1965 (Act No. 55 of 1965), shall mutatis mutandis apply in respect of the expropriation of any land in terms of subsection (1).

(3) If the Minister of Agriculture expropriates land as aforesaid for the benefit of the council, the council shall become the owner thereof and the expropriation notice of the Minister of Agriculture shall be deemed to authorize the registrar of deeds concerned to register the transfer of such land to the council.

(4) All costs lawfully incurred by the said Minis ters in the execution of their duties in terms of this section, shall be refunded to them by the council.".
