# Usury (previously Limitation and Disclosure of Finance Charges) Amendment Act 76 of 1970

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Limitation and Disclosure of Finance Charges Act, 1968, relating to definitions, finance charge rates, disclosure of finance charges, reduction of instalments and furnishing or copies or instruments of debt. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 29th September, 1970.)
