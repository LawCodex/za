# Immorality Amendment Act 57 of 1969

ACT

To amend the prOVISIOns of the Immorality Act, 1957, with regard to the commission of offences with girls or boys;

to prohibit the manufacture, sale or supply of any article . which is intended to be used to perform an unnatural sexual act; to make provision in the said Act for a prohibition on the commission at a party of acts between male persons and which are calculated to stimulate sexual passion or to give sexual gratification; and to repeal section 10 of Act No. 22 of 1898 of Natal.
