# Community Development Act 3 of 1966

ACT

To consolidate the law relating to the development of certain areas, the promotion of community development in such areas, the control of the disposal of affected properties, the grant of assistance to persons to acquire or hire immovable property, the establishment for such purposes of a board and the definition of its functions, and matters incidental thereto.
