# Transfer Duty Amendment Act 59 of 1951

ACT

· To amend the Transfer Duty Act, 1949.

(Afrikaans text signed by the Governor-Genera/.) (Assented to 21th June, 1951.)

BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Union of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section two of the Transfer Duty Act, 1949, is hereby amended by the substitution for the words "two pounds" · of the words "three pounds".

2\. The amendment effected by section one shall be deemed to have come into operation on the twenty-second day of March, 1951, and shall apply in respect of any acquisition of property· or any renunciation of an interest in or restriction upon the use or disposal of property on or after that date.

3\. This Act shall be called the Transfer Duty Amendment Act, 1951.
