The Remuneration of Town Clerks Act, 1984 (Act No. 115 of 1984). the Remuneration of Town Clerks Amendment Act, 1986 (Act No. 96 of 1986), the Remuneration of Town Clerks Amendment Act, 1987 (Act No. 106 of 1987), and the Remuneration of Town Clerks· Amendment Act, 1990 (Act No. 26 of 1990), are hereby repealed.

#### 2. Transitional arrangements

2\. The Minister for Provincial Affairs and Constitutional ,Development may after such consultation as he or she may deem fit, including consultation with the person who holds or held the office referred to in section 2(2) of the Remuneration of Town Clerks, 1984 (Act No. 115 of 1984 ), make such arrangements as he or she may deem necessary for the finalisation of any matter, including financial matters, connected with the functions of the Board on the Remuneration and Service Benefits of Town Clerks established by section 2(1) of the said Act.

#### 3. Short title

3\. This Act shall be called the Remuneration of Town Clerks Act, Repeal Act, 1996.
