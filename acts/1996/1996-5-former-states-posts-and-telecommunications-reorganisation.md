# Former States Posts and Telecommunications Reorganisation Act 5 of 1996

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with .a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To provide for the integration of the departments of posts and telecommunications of the former Republics of Transkei, Bophuthatswana, Venda and Ciskei with Telkom SA Limited and the South African Post Office Limited; and to provide for the transfer of the postal and the telecommunications enterprises conducted by those department<; as well as certain land used by them for that purpose to the said companies; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
