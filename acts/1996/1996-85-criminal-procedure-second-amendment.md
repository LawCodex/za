# Criminal Procedure Second Amendment Act 85 of 1996

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined ·with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Criminal Procedure Act, 1977, so as to regulate the setting of traps and the engaging in undercover operations; to detennine the circumstances under which evidence so obtained will be admissible as evidence; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.

(English text signed by the President.) · (Assented to 6 November 1996.).

#### 1. B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Insertion of section 252A in Act 51 of 1977

1\. The following section is hereby inserted in the Criminal Procedure Act, 1977 (Act No. 51 of 1977), after section 252:

"Authority to make use of traps and undercover operations and admissibility of evidence so obtained

252A. ( 1) Any law enforcement officer, official of the State or any other person authorised thereto for such purpose (hereinafter referred to in this section as an official or his or her agent) may make use of a trap or engage in an undercover operation in order to detect,' investigate or uncover the commission of an offence, or to prevent the commission of any offence, and the evidence so obtained shall be admissible if that conduct does not go - beyond providing an opportunity to commit an offence: Provided that \vhere the conduct goes beyond providing an opportunity to commit an offence a court may admit evidence so obtained subject to subsection (3).
