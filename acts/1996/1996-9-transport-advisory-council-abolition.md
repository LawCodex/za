#### 1. Abolition of Transport Advisory Council

1\. The Transport Advisory Council established by section 2 of the Transport Advisory Council Act, 1987 (Act No. 58 of 1987). is hereby abolished. 5

#### 2. Repeal of laws

2\. The Transport Advisory Council Act, 1987 (Act No. 58 of 1987), sections 37 and 38 of the Trans net Limited Amendment Act, 1991 (Act No. 52 of 1991 ), and sections 16 to 25 of the Transport General Amendment Act, 1992 (Act No. 86 of 1992), are hereby repealed. 10

#### 3. Short title ~nd commence~ent

3\. This Act shall be called the Transport Advisory Council Abolition Act. 1996, and shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 April 1995.
