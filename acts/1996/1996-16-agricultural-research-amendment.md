# Agricultural Research Amendment Act 16 of 1996

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing en-actments.

To amend the Agricultural Research Act, 1990t so as to reregulate the constitution of the Council managing the affairs of the Agricultural Research Council; to further regulate the period of office of members of the Council; and to replace an obsolete provision; to extend the application of the Act; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
