# Education Affairs Amendment Act (House of Assembly) 36 of 1993

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Education Affairs Act (House of Assembly), 1988, so as to define or further define certain expressions; to further regulate the transfer of immovable property to state-aided schools; to alter the financial year of state-aided schools; to regulate the closure of state-aided schools; to effect certain textual alterations; and to further regulate the determination of certain establishments; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
