# Marketing Amendment Act 188 of 1993

ACT

To amend the Marketing Act, 1968, so as to further regulate the revocation of schemes; and to further provide for the orderly dispensation of money gained through levies; to repeal the Egg Production Control Act, 1970; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 3. Repeal of Acts

3\. The Egg Production Control Act, 1970 (Act No. 61 of 1970), and the Egg Production Control Amendment Act, 1980 (Act No. 81 of 1980), are hereby repealed.

#### 4. Short title

4\. This Act shall be called the Marketing Amendment Act, 1993.
