#### 3. Insertion of section 2A in Act 71 of 1962

3\. The following section is hereby inserted after section 2 of the Animals Protection Act, 1962: ·

"Animal fights

(a) possesses, keeps, imports, buys, sells, trains, breeds or has under his control an animal for the purpose of fighting any other animal;
