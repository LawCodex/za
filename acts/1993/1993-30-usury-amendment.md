# Usury Amendment Act 30 of 1993

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets. indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments. .

ACT

To amend the Usury Act, 1968, so as to amend certain definitions; to further regulate the levying of finance charges; to abolish certain exemptions regarding disclosure of finance charges in respect of certain money lending transactions and debentures; to further regulate the furnishing of certain information to borrowers, credit receivers and lessees; and to provide for the appointment of inspectors; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
