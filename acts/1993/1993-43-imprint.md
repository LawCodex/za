1\. In this Act, unless the context otherwise indicates-

"Minister" means the Minister of Home Affairs;

"prescribe" means prescribe by regulation;

"printed matter" means any picture or any mark conveying any meaning which has been affixed on any material, or has been produced by impressing or transferring other material thereon;

"printer" means any person carrying on or managing a business which produces printed matter.
