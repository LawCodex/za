# Sectional Titles Amendment Act 15 of 1993

ACT

To amend the Sectional Titles Act, 1986, so as to emend the definition of

"Minister"; to provide that a lessee of a part of a building in terms of a lease agreement concluded with a previous owner of the building will continue to enjoy the same protection when the building is bought by a developer; to delete the provision in terms of which the Minister may determine certain fees to be paid to architects and land surveyors; and to provide for the alienation and mortgaging of a right to extend a building in terms of the Sectional Titles Act, ·1971; and to provide for matters connected therewith. · (English text signed by the State President.)
