# Railways and Harbours Acts Amendment Act 24 of 1971

ACT

To amend section 7 of the Railway Expropriation Act, 1955, and sections 2, 3, 26, 40, 54, 58 and 70 of ·the Railways and Harbours Control and Management· (Consolidation) Act, .. 1957, so as to provide for conversion to the metric system of weights and measures; to amend section 1 of the Railways and Harbours Control and Management (Consolidation) Act, 1957, so as to extend the definition of "railways" to include any area of land within the limits of an airport which is used by the Administration for the purposes of its air services; to amend section 8 of the Railways and Har bours Superannuation Fund Act, 1960, so as to provide for a reduction in the contributions of members of the New Railways and Harbours Superaunuation Fund to that Fund;

to amend section 2 of the Level Crossings Act, 1960, so as to provide · for an increase in contributions towards the Level Crossings Elimination Fund; to validate certain changes in conditions of employment; and to provide for other incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 29th March, 1971.) .

9\. Section 70 of the Control and ManagementActis hereby amended by the substitution, in subsection ( 4)bis, for the words

"twelve rand per mile" of the words "seven rand fifty cents per kilometre".

10\. (1) Section 8 of the Railways and Harbours Super annuation Fund Act, 1960 (Act No. 39 of 1960), is hereby amended-

(a) by the substitution for subsection (1) of the following subsection:

"(1) Every member who is an officer shall, so long as he remains in the Service, contribute to the New Fund four per cent of his pensionable emoluments.";

(b) by the substitution for subsection (4) of the following subsection:

"(4) Every member who is an employee shall, so long as he remains in the Service, contribute to the New Fund four per cent of his pensionable emoluments.".

(2) Subsection (1) (a) of this section shall come into operation on the first day of April, 1971, and sub section (I) (b) thereof shall be deemed to have come into operation on the sixteenth day of March, 1971.

11\. Section 2 of the Level Crossings Act, 1960 (Act No. 41 of 1960), is hereby amended by the substitution, in subsection (2), for the words "three million rand" of the words "four million five hundred thousand rand".

12\. All changes in conditions of employment for which provision is made in any regulation published under any Government Notice mentioned in the Schedule to this Act, and which were brought into operation with retrospective effect or in respect of which the amending regulations relating thereto were not approved by the State President within the period of three months mentioned in section 32 (3) of the Railways and Harbours Service Act, 1960 (Act No. 22 of 1960), are hereby validated with effect from the several dates of commencement of such changes in terms of the relevant Government Notices.

13\. This Act and any amendment thereof shall, with the exception of section 11 thereof, apply also in the territory of South-West Africa, including the Eastern Caprivi Zipfel.

14\. This Act shall be called the Railways and Harbours Acts Amendment Act, 1971, and, subject to the provisions of section 10 (2), shall come into operation on the first day of April, 1971.
