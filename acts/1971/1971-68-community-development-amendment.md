# Community Development Amendment Act 68 of 1971

ACT

To amend the Community Development Act, 1966, to extend the period upon the expiry of which appreciation contributions shall become payable.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to lOth June, 1971.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and . the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-
