# State Tender Board and State Procurement Board Amendment Act 74 of 1971

ACT

To amend the provisions of the· State Tender Board and State Procurement Board Act, 1968, relating to the definitions;

the powers of the State Tender Board; and the delegation of its powers by the State Tender Board; to provide for the abolition of the State Procurement Board; and to provide for incidental matters.
