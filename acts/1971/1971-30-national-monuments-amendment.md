# National Monuments Amendment Act 30 of 1971

ACT

To amend the provisions of the National Monuments Act, 1969, relating to the declaration of immovable property to be national monuments.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 30th April, 1971.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section 10 of the National Monuments Act, 1969, is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (c) of sub section (3) of the following paragraph:

"(c) that, in the case of movable property, such property has been in the Republic for more than fifty years.".

2\. This Act shall be called the National Monuments Amend ment Act, 1971.
