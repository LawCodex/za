# Disaster Management Act 57 of 2002

ACT

To provide for- * an integrated and co-ordinated disaster management policy that focuses on preventing or reducing the risk of disasters, mitigating the severity of disasters, emergency preparedness, rapid and effective response to disasters and post-disaster recovery;

\* the establishment of national, provincial and municipal disaster management centres;

\* disaster management volunteers; and * matters incidental thereto.
