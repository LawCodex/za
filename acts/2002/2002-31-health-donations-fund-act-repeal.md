# Health Donations Fund Act Repeal Act 31 of 2002

ACT

To provide for the disestablishment of the Health Donations Fund and for the repeal of the Health Donations Fund Act, 1978; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Disestablishment of Health Donations Fund .-

1\. (1) The Health Donations Fund established by section 2 of the Health Donations Fund Act, 1978 (Act No. 11 of 1978), is hereby disestablished. 5
