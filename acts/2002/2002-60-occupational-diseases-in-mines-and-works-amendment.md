# Occupational Diseases in Mines and Works Amendment Act 60 of 2002

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 30 Decemker 2002.)

ACT

To amend the Occupational Diseases in Mines and Works Act, 1973, so as to provide that if a person was medically examined within a period of 24 months immediately preceding an application for medical examination, the Director of the Medical Bureau for Occupational Diseases may refuse that person’s application for medical examination; and to provide for matters incidental thereto. B EIT EXACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 32 of Act 78 of 1973

1\. Section 32 of the Occupational Diseases in Mines and Works Act, 1973 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), is amended by the substitution for subsection (3) of the following subsection:

“(3) The director may [in his discretion] refuse such application if the person concerned was medically examined under this Act within the period of [six] 24 months immediately preceding the date on which such application is received. unless the application is supported in writing by a medical practitioner.”. 10 Amendment of section 36A of Act 78 of 1973, as amended by section 11 of Act 208 of 1993

2\. Section 36A of the principal Act is amended by the substitution for subsection (1) of the following subsection:

“( 1) The owner of a controlled mine or a controlled works shall [for a period of not more than two years] from the date of the commencement of a compensatable disease pay the [reasonable] legitimate and proven cost incurred by or on behalf of a person in his orher service, or who was in his or her service at the commencement of a compensatable disease, in respect of medical aid necessitated by such disease.”. 20 Amendment of section 124 of Act 78 of 1973, as amended by section 43 of Act 208 of 1993
