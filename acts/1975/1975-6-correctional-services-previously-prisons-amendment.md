GOVERNMENT GAZETIE, 12 MARCH 1975 PRISONS AMENDMENT ACf, 1975.

ACT· To amend the provisions of the Prisons Act, 1959, in order to distinguish between chaplains on the fixed establishment of the Prisons Department and other persons ministering to the spiritual needs in prisons.

------,- (English text siined by the State President.) (Assented to 6 March 1975.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Mrica, as follows:-

1\. The following section is hereby substituted for section 7 of the Prisons Act, 1959:

"Ministers 7. (l) Apart from commissioned officers appoint of religion. ed under section 4 (1} who hold posts as chaplains on the fixed establishment of the Prisons Department, the Minister may from time to time grant written permission to" one· or more ministers of religion or such other persons as he may deem fit, to render such services and to perform such functions, in respect of any prison or group of prisons, as may be speci fied in the letters in which permission is so granted to such ministers of religion or other persons.

. (2) Any permission granted by the Minister under the provisions of this section may at any time be cancelled by him.".

2\. This Act shall be called the Prisons Amendment Act, 1975.
