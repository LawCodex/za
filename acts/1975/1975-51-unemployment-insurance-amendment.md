# Unemployment Insurance Amendment Act 51 of 1975

ACT

To amend the Unemployment Insurance Act, 1966, so as to in crease the maximum earnings in respect of which contri butions are payable; to reduce the period within which certain contributions or other payments due, or purporting to have been paid, to the fund may be recovered; to reduce the period for which certain prescribed records are to be retained; and to provide for incidental matters.
