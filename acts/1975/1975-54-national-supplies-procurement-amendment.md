NATIONAL SUPPLIES PROCUREMENT AMENDMENT ACT, 1975.

ACT:

To amend the provisions of the National Supplies Procurement Act, 1970, so as to further regulate the powers of the Minister in respect of goods; to provide for the liability of employers and principals for certain acts and omissions of their mana gers, employees or agents, the admissibility of certain state ments and records in prosecutions for offences under the said Act and the application of a certain presumption in certain prosecutions; to amend certaht provisions of certain Acts with reference to the application of such provisions in respect of certain offences and sentences; to authorize the Minister to declare the provisions of certain notices to be applicable to persons in the employment of the State acting in the performance of their duties, and to grant certain exemptions; and to provide for incidental matters.

16B. (1) At the trial of any person charged with an offence under this Act, any statement or record contained in any book or document kept by the accused or any employee or agent of the accused, in respect of any goods or service specified in a notice under section 2 (b), shall be admissible in evidence against the accused as an admission of ·the facts set out in that statement or record, unless it is proved that the statement or record was not made by such person or his employee or agent.

(2) If in any prosecution for a contravention of a prohibition imposed under section 2 (b) relating to the use of goods specified in the charge, it is proved that the accused performed an act which would have constituted the contravention if such goods had been used for the performance of that act, it shall be presumed unless the contrary is proved, that the accused used such goods for the performance of that act. ·

16C. (1) In the application of section 258, 259, 309bis or 351 of the Criminal Procedure Act, 1955 (Act No. 56 of 1955), in respect of an offence under this Act, a reference in the section in question to an amount of one hundred rand shall be construed as a reference to an amount of five hundred rand.

(2) In the application of section 96 of the Magi strates' Courts Act, 1944 (Act No. 32 of 1944), to a sentence in a criminal case in respect of an offence under this Act, a reference to an amount of two hundred and fifty rand shall be construed as a refer ence to an amount of five hundred rand.

16D. (1) The Minister may by notice in the Gazette declare a notice under section 2 (b), or one or more of the provisions thereof, to be applicable to any person in the employment of the State acting in the performance of his duties .

(2) A notice under subsection (1) may grant exemption from the provisions thereof or may provide for the granting of such exemption.".

3\. This Act shall be called the National Supplies Procurement Amendment Act, 1975.
