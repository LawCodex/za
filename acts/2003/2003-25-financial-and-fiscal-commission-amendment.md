# Financial and Fiscal Commission Amendment Act 25 of 2003

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enadtments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 12 October 2003.)

ACT

To amend the Financial and Fiscal Commission Act, 1997, so as to extend the functions of the Financial and Fiscal Commission; to bring the number, and appointment procedure, of members of the Commission into line with section 221 of the Constitution; and to alter certain procedures in respect of financial matters in order to bring the said Act into line with the Public Finance Management Act, 1999; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
