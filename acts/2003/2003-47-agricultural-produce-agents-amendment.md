GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Agricultural Produce Agents Act, 1992, so as to alter certain definitions and to insert others; to change the constitution of the council; to apply certain provisions to fresh produce agents only: to make certain textual alterations; to provide for the extension of the jurisdiction of the council, to enhance the regulating powers of the council: and to provide for transitional matters; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
