ACT

To amend the National Development Agency Act, 1998, so as to amend the definition of Minister; to reduce the number of members on the Board; to further regulate meetings of the Board; to empower the Minister to appoint the chief executive officer; and to further regulate delegation: and to provide for matters connected therewith.
