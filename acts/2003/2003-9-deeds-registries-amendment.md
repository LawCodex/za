# Deeds Registries Amendment Act 9 of 2003

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 23 April 2003.)

ACT

To amend the Deeds Registries Act, 1937, so as to make further provision regarding the registration of immovable property in the names of persons married under the Recognition of Customary Marriages Act, 1998; and to provide fofthe registration of immovable property in the name of a trust; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
