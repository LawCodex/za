# Pensions (Supplementary) Act 8 of 2003

ACT

To provide for the award of certain pension benefits.

#### 1. B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Award of certain pension benefits

1\. There shall be paid to the person mentioned in the Schedule the pension benefits mentioned therein. - 5

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Pensions (Supplementary) Act, 2003. Act No. 8,2003 PENSIONS (SUPPLEMENTARY) ACT, 2003 ..

## SCHEDULE

There shall be paid from the National Revenue Fund to Graham Christian Clarke:

(1) arrear additional pension for a period of six months from 1 September 2001 to 28

(2) an additional monthly pension of R1500 per month, with effect from 15 March February 2002 in an amount of R1500 per month; and 2002.
