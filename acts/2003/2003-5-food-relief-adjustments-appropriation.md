# Food Relief Adjustments Appropriation Act 5 of 2003

ACT

To appropriate additional amounts of money for the requirements of the Department of Social Development and the Department of Foreign Affairs in respect of the 2002/03 financial year.

#### 1. B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa. as fol1ows:- Appropriation of additional amounts of money for requirements of Department of Social Development and Department of Foreign Affairs

1\. Subject to the Public Finance Management Act. 1999 (Act No. 1 of 1999), there is hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of-

(a) the Department of Social Development, an amount of R230 million (two hundred and thirty million rand) in respect of the 3002/03 financial year to, fund the implementation of domestic food relief interventions; and 07) the Department of Foreign Affairs. an amount of R170 million (one hundred' 10 and seventy million rand) in respect of the 2002/03 finankial year to give effect to the Republic of South Africa's commitment to assist in the provision of food relief to countries in the SADC region most seriously affected by the food crisis.

#### 2. Short title 1s

2\. This Act ih called the Food Relief Adjustments Appropriation Act, 2003
