# Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation Act Repeal Act 14 of 2003

ACT

To repeal the Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation'Act, 1989 (Bophuthatswana); and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 1. B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Disestablishment of Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation

1\. The Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation established by section 2 of the Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation Act, 1989 (Act No. 37 of 1989) 5 (Bophuthatswana), is hereby disestablished.

#### 2. Repeal of law

2\. The Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation Act, 1989 (Act No. 37 of 1989) (Bophuthatswana), is hereby repealed.

#### 3. Short title and commencement

3\. This Act is called the Sefalana Employee Benefits Organisation Act Repeal Act, 2003, and takes effect on a date fixed by the President by proclamation in the Gazette.
