# Adjustments Appropriation Act 37 of 2003

ACT

To appropriate adjusted amounts of money for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 2004.

#### 1. EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as B follows:- Appropriation of adjusted amounts of money for requirements of State

1\. Subject to the Public Finance Management Act, 1999 (Act No. I of 1999), there are hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending on 3 1 March 2004, the adjusted amounts of money shown in the Schedule.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Adjustments Appropriation Act, 2003 Act No. 37,2003 ADJUSTMENTS APPROPRIATION ACT, 2003
