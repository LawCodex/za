# National Small Business Amendment Act 26 of 2003

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 21 November 2003.)

ACT

To amend the National Small Business Act, 1996, so as to repeal all provisions pertaining to the National Small Business Council; to empower the Minister to facilitate and establish an Advisory Body to represent the interests of small business; to make the Public Finance Management Act, 1999, applicable to the Agency; to make further provision in respect of the functions of the Agency; to do away with the requirement of calling for nominations for directors of the Board by notice in the Gazette; to repeal the provisions pertaining to alternate directors; and to substitute the Schedule to that Act, in order to reflect the current economic position; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
