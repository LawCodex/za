# Higher Education Amendment Act 38 of 2003

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 10 December 2003.)

ACT

To amend the Higher Education Act, 1997, so as to provide for the regular reporting by the Council on Higher Education on the state of higher education; to provide for consequential changes arising out of the incorporation of public higher education institutions in relation to labour and student matters; to provide for the establishment of a National Institute for Higher Education in Mpumalanga and a National Institute for Higher Education in the Northern Cape to coordinate the regional provision of higher education; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
