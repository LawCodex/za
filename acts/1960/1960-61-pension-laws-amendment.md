# Pension Laws Amendment Act 61 of 1960

ACT

To amend the Government Service Pensions Act, 1955; to give an option to certain persons to include certain periods of employment in their pensionable service; to provide for the payment of bonuses to persons in .receipt of certain pensions, grants or payments and· for the repeal of certain existing provisions relating to payment of bonuses to such persons;

and to provide for other incidental matters.
