## Schedule 1

I Definition of Refined Sugar Short title and commencement.

"Refined sugar" means sugar which complies substantially with the following specifications: Polarization, not less than 99,CJ' S;

Conductivity Ash (5° Bx solution), not greater than 0,03%:

ICUMSA Colour, not more than 125 (solution is not filtered nor de-aerated but left to stand for one hour prior to colour measurement).

## Schedule 2

Definition of Speciality Sugar

"Speciality sugar" means any of the following products, namely: Cube, superfine dessen, icing, castor, white crystal, coloured crystal, coffee crystal, extra fine, micro fine, superfine baking, confectioners, sanding, canners, caramel brown, soft brown, treacle, demarara. candy, invert sugar, golden syrup, treacle syrup, the production of which involves processing costs other than the nonnal milling and refining costs.
