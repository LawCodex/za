# National Welfare Amendment Act 77 of 1978

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments. ·

ACT

To amend the National Welfare Act, 1965, so as to repeal the provisions relating to the circumstances under which persons may not be registered as social workers; and to create an additional offence.

(English text signed by theState President.) (Assented to 29 May 1978.) .
