# National Minimum Wage Amendment Act 3 of 2020

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the National Minimum Wage Act, 2018, so as to correct a crossreference; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Amendment of section 17 of Act 9 of 2018

1\. Section 17 of the National Minimum Wage Act, 2018, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (4) of the following subsection:

“(4) Section [4(6)] 4(8) takes retrospective effect from 1 May 2017.”.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the National Minimum Wage Amendment Act, 2020. (English text signed by the President) (Assented to 5 August 2020)
