2\. Nothing in this Act shall-

(a) affect the previous operation of any law repealed hereby or anything duly done or suffered under any law so repealed ;

(b) affect any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under any law so repealed;

(c) affect any penalty, forfeiture or punishment incurl'ed in respect of any offence committed against any la.w so repealed ; .

(d) affect any question whether-

(i) any law not expressly repealed by this Act has in fact been repealed ; or (ii) any law expressly repealed by this Act was in fact in force at .the commencement of this Act;

(e) deprive any person of any of the benefits conferred upon him by any indemnity Act repealed hereby.

3\. This Act shall be called the Orange Free State Statute Law Revision Act, 1936.
