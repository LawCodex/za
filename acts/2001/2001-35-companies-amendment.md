# Companies Amendment Act 35 of 2001

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

r I Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactment.3.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 28 November 2001.) To amend the Companies Act, 1973, so as to allow the electronic lodgement of and access to all documents which have been or are required to be lodged in terms of this Act; to provide for the payment of fees in respect of documents so lodged; to further regulate the acquisition by a company of its own shares; to amend certain obsolete provisions; and to effect certain textual alterations; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
