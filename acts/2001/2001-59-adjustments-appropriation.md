# Adjustments Appropriation Act 59 of 2001

ACT

To appropriate adjusted amounts of money for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 2002.

#### 1. B follow^:- EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as Appropriation of adjusted amounts of money for requirements of State

1\. Subject to the Public Finance Management Act, 1999 (Act No. 1 of 1999), there are hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending on 3 1 March 2002, the adjusted amounts of money shown in column “Vote” of column 1 of the Schedule.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Adjustments Appropriation Act, 2001.
