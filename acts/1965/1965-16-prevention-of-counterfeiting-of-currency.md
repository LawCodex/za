# Prevention of Counterfeiting of Currency Act 16 of 1965

ACT

To provide for the prevention of the counterfeiting of coin and the forging or altering of certain bank notes and for matters incidental thereto, to provide for certain persons being dealt with under the Jaw relating to extradition, and to amend the Native Territories Penal Code (Act No. 24 of 1886 of the Cape of Good Hope), the Crimes Ordinance, 1904, of the Transvaal, the South African Reserve Bank Act, 1944, and the Criminal Procedure Act, 1955.
