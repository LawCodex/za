# Wills Amendment Act 41 of 1965

ACT

To amend the Wills Act, 1953.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 2nd April, 1965.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section two of the Wills Act, 1953, is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-section (I) for the words preceding paragraph (a) of the following words:

"Subject to the provisions of sections three and three bis-".
