# Police Second Amendment Act 118 of 1992

====--=========-----====== -·-··

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to prohibit members of the Reserve Police Force, only while serving in the Force, to take part in the activities of political parties or any other movements, organizations, bodies or associations with political objects, and to prohibit a member of Parliament from performing service in the Force as a member of the Reserve Police Force or Police Reserve; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
