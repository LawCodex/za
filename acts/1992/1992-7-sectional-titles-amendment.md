# Sectional Titles Amendment Act 7 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Sectional Titles Act, 1986, so as to substitute certain definitions and define certain expressions more closely; to further regulate certain matters regarding development schemes, sectional plans and sectional title registers; to rectify an expression; to further regulate proof of payment of rates and moneys to the local authority; to further regulate a notice to the registrar given at the substitution, addition to, amendment or repeal of management and conduct rules; and to provide for the payment by the developer to the body corporate of any residue of revenue; and to provide for incidental matters.
