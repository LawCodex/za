# Referendums Amendment Act 97 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ l Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Referendums Act, 1983, so as to extend the definition of voter; to regulate explicitly certain aspects relating to the holding of a referendum and the appointment of a chief referendum officer; and to adjust the power of the Minister of Home Affairs in relation to the making of regulations on prior votes; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
