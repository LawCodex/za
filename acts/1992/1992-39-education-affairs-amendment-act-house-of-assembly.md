# Education Affairs Amendment Act (House of Assembly) 39 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Act No. 39, 1992

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Education Affairs Act (House of Assembly), 1988, so as to redefine an expression; to provide for the establishment of a single management council for more than one public school; to further regulate the declaration of certain public schools as state-aided schools; to provide for the establishment of a state-aided school; to further regulate the transfer of immovable property to state-aided schools; to· provide that a person who is suspended from his service, may, with the permission of the Minister, assume duty in another position during the period of suspension; to further regulate the transfer of staff from a public school to a state-aided school; and to provide for the promulgation of regulations as to the receipt and use of funds or other goods by centres and educational auxiliary services; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
