#### 1. Amendment of section 4 of Act 139 of 1991

1\. Section 4 of the Prevention of Public Violence and Intimidation Act, 1991, 5 is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraphs (b) and (c) of the following paragraphs:

"(b) A committee shall consist of such number of persons[, but not less than three,] as may be determined by the Commission [, and of whom at least one shall he a member of the Commission].

10 (c) The Commission shall designate a member of a committee [who is also, a member of the Commission,] as chnirman of the committee.".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Prevention of Public Violence and Intimidation Amendment Act. 1992.
