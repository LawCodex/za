# Divorce Amendment Act 44 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Divorce Act, 1979, so as to confer certain powers upon a court in the case where such court grants a decree of divorce in respect of a marriage the patrimonial consequences of which are governed by the law of a foreign state. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 7 Apri/1992.) B EIT ENACfED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 7 of Act 70 of 1979, as amended by section 36 of Act 88 of 1984, section 2 of Act 3 of 1988 and section 2 of Act 7 of 1989 5 1. Section 7 of the Divorce Act, 1979, is hereby amended by the addition of the following subsection:

" 9 When a court rants a decree of divorce in res ect of a marria e the patrimonial consequences of which are according to the rules of the South African private international law governed by the law of a foreign state, the 10 court shall have the same power as a competent court of the foreign state concerned would have had at that time to order that assets be transferred from one spouse to the other spouse.".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Divorce Amendment Act, 1992.
