# Abolition of Racially Based Land Measures Amendment Act 133 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Abolition of Racially Based Land Measures Act, 1991, so as to make further provision for the extension of the period within which the registrar of deeds shall be authorized to register land acquired on behalf of a principal by a nominee owner, in the name of the principal.

(Aji·ikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 2 July 1992.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 48 of Act 108 of 1991 I. Section 48 of the Abolition of Racially Based Land Measures Act, 1991, is 5 hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (3) of the following subsec tion:

"(3) The parties to a transaction referred to in subsection (2) may within

[six] thirty months after the commencement of this section in writing request the registrar of deeds concerned to transfer property which by virtue 10 of the transaction is registered in the name of the nominee owner to the principal in accordance with this section.''.

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act shall be called the Abolition of Racially Based Land Measures Amendment Act, J 992, and shall be deemed to have come into operation on 15 1 January 1992.
