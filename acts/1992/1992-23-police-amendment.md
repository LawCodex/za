# Police Amendment Act 23 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to further regulate appointments and filling of posts in the South African Police; to make provision for the retention of their commissions by ex-officers; to repeal the provisions regarding the prohibition of the publication of certain untrue statements; and to further regulate the powers of police officials of certain states and territories, for the purposes of actions in the Republic in terms of agreements with such states or territories; and to provide for incidental matters.
