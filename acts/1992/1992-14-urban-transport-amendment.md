# Urban Transport Amendment Act 14 of 1992

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

( ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Urban Transport Act, 1977, in order to effect a technical improvement; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 3 March 1992.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 13 of Act 78 of 1977, as amended by section 8 of Act 77 of 5 1. Section 13 of the Urban Transport Act, 1977, is hereby amended by the substitution in the Afrikaans text for paragraph (f) of subsection (1) of the following paragraph:

"(f) hoogstens [twee] drie lede benoem deur die liggame wat na die mening van die betrokke Administrateur verteenwoordigend is 10 van openbare vervoeroperateurs, met inbegrip van openbare vervoeroperateurs wat passasiers in die betrokke stedelike ver voergebied vir huur of beloning vervoer;".

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act shall be called the Urban Transport Amendment Act, 1992, and 15 shall be deemed to have come into operation on the date of commencement of the Urban Transport Amendment Act, 1990 (Act No. 77 of 1990).
