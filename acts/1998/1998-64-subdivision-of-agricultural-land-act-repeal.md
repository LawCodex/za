# Subdivision of Agricultural Land Act Repeal Act 64 of 1998

ACT

To repeal certain laws pertaining to subdivision of agricultural land; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

---. ,.. .,., ---- -.“, (English text signed by the Acting President.) (Assented to 16 September 1998.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, asfollows:—

#### 1. Repeal of laws

1\. The laws mentioned in the Schedule are repealed to the extent set out in the third column thereof. 5

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act is called the Subdivision of Agricultural Land Act Repeal Act, 1997, and comes into operation on a date fixed by the President by proclamation in the Gazetfe. Act No. 64, 1998 SUBDIVISION OF AGRICULTURAL LAND ACT REPEAL ACT, 1998
