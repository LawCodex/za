# Agricultural Product Standards Amendment Act 63 of 1998

ACT

To amend the Agricultural Product Standards Act, 1990, so as to insert and substitute certain definitions; to furtherregulate the appointment of assignees; to make provision for the charging of certain fees; to make provision for quality control; to make further provision for exemption from export standards and requirements; to make further provision for imported products; to make further provision for distinctive marks; to provide for the use of geographical or other names; to further regulate provisions relating to powers of entry, investigation, sampling and seizure; to provide for disclosure of information to protect the consumer;toamendappeals;toadjustpenalties;andtomakefurtherprovisionfor matters to be prescribed; and provide formatters connected therewith.
