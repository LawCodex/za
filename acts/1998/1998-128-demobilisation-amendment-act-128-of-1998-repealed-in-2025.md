ACT

To amend the Demobilisation Act, 1996, so as to amend certain definitions; to further regulate the payment of a dependant’s benefit; and to effect certain technical amendments; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 1. BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 1 of Act 99 of 1996

1\. Section I of the Demobilisation Act. 1996 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), is hereby amended- 5

(a) by the substitution for the definition of “closing date” of the following definition:

“ ‘closing date’. for the purposes of section [6(l)(c)] S(I)(c), means [the date 12 months after the date on which this Act comes into operation] 31 March 1999;“: and IO

(b) by the substitution in the definition of “dependant” for the words preceding paragraph (0) of the following words:

‘- ‘dependant’, for the purposes of section [5] I, includes-“.

#### 2. Amendment of section 3 of Act 99 of 1996

2\. Section 3 of the principal Act is hereby amended- 15

(a) by the substitution for paragraph (0) of subsection (I) of the following paragraph:

“(I>) determine whether an applicant is eligible to benefit under the demobilisation programme in terms of sections [4 and 515 and 7;“; and (0) by the substitution for paragraph (e) of subsection (I) of the following paragraph:

“(e) determine the validity of the mandate of any person to act on behalf of the applicant referred to in section [6(2)] 8(2); and”.

#### 3. Amendment of section 7 of Act 99 of 1996

3\. Section 7 of the principal Act is hereby amended- 25

(a) hy the substitution for subsections (I) and (2) of the following subsections, respectively:

“(I) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, a surviving dependant of any person who, but for his or her death, would have been 4 No. 19543 GOVERNMENT GAZE-II-E. 2 DECEMBER 1998 Act No. 128.1998 DEMOBILISATION AMENDMENT ACT, I998 eligible for demobilisation in terms of section [4] 5 shall, subject to subsection (3). be entitled to receive the demobilisation gratuity to which that person would have been entitled, had he or she survived.

(2) For the purposes of this section, section [4(c)] 5(I)(c) shall not apply in determining the eligibility of the deceased.“; and (b) by the deletion of subsection (4).

#### 4. Short title and commencement

4\. This Act is called the Demobilisation Amendment Act, 1998, and shall be deemed to have come into operation on 18 February 1997.
