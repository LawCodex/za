# Agricultural Laws Rationalisation Act 72 of 1998

ACT

To provide for the rationalisation of certain laws relating to agricultural affairs that remained in force in various areas of the national territory of the Republic prior to the commencement of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa; and to provide formatters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Definitions

1\. In thisAct, unless the context otherwise indicates—

“remainderofthenationalterritory”meanstheareaswhichcomprisedtheformer Republics of Transkei, Bophuthatswana, Venda and Ciskei and the areas which comprised the former self-governing territories excluding the national territory of the former Republic of SouthAfrica;

“Republic”means the national territory of the former Republic of South Africa prior to the commencement of the Constitution excluding the remainder of the national territory.

#### 2. Extension of application of certain laws

2\. (1) The laws mentioned in Schedule 1, as they were in force in the Republic immediately prior to the commencement of this Act, will, from a date fixed by the President by proclamation in theGazette, also apply in the remainder of the national territory.

(2)The law mentioned in Schedule 2 as it was in force in the Republic immediately prior to the commencement of this Act, will, from a date fixed by the President by proclamation in theGazette, also apply to the territory of the former Republic of Transkei.

#### 3. Repeal of laws, and savings

3\. (1) Subject to the provisions of this section, the laws mentioned in the second column of Schedule 3 are repealed to the extent that such laws were in force in the various areas of the remainder of the national territory as set out in the fourth column. (2)Anythingdoneunderalawrepealedintermsofsubsection(1)whichiscapableof being done under a provision of a law mentioned in the second column of Schedule 3, will be deemed to have been done under such provision of such law. Short title 4.ThisAct is called theAgricultural Laws RationalisationAct, 1998.
