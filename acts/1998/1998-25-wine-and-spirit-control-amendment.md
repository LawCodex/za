# Wine and Spirit Control Amendment Act 25 of 1998

ACT

To amend the Wine and Spirit Control Act, 1970, so as to insert and to delete certain definitions; to authorise the utilisation of the research levy for additional purposes; to provide for the control and management of the wine and spirit industry during a transitional phase; to establish a management authority; to authorisethedesignationofassigneestoenforceandadministerthatActorcertain provisions thereof; to make certain textual alterations; and to provide for the lapsingofthatActattheendofthetransitionalphase;andtoprovideformatters connected therewith.

#### 1. Establishment of management authority

1\. There is hereby established a juristic person to be known as the management authority, with the capacity to sue and be sued in its own name.

Meetings and recommendations of management authority 3.(1)Themanagementauthorityshallmeetforthefirsttimeatthetime andplacedeterminedbytheMinisterandthereafteratthetimesandplaces determined by the chairperson or, if he or she is absent, the vicechairperson.
