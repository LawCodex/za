# Appropriation Act 29 of 1998

ACT

ToappropriateamountsofmoneyfortherequirementsoftheStateinrespectofthe financial yearending 31 March 1999.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Appropriation of amounts of money forrequirements of State

1\. Subject to the provisions of the ExchequerAct, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the Stateinrespectofthefinancialyearending31March1999,theamountsofmoneyasset out in column 1 of the Schedule.

Short title 2.ThisAct is called theAppropriationAct, 1998.
