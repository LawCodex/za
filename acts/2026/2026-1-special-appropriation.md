# Special Appropriation Act 1 of 2026

ACT

To appropriate an additional amount of money for the requirements of the vote of Health for the 2025/26 financial year to address the shortfall resulting from the withdrawal of international donor funding to the health sector; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Appropriation of additional amount of money for requirements of vote of Health

1\. (1) An amount of R754.527 million is hereby appropriated from the National Revenue Fund for expenditure authorised in terms of section 16(1) of the Public Finance Management Act, 1999 (Act No. 1 of 1999), for the 2025/26 financial year, and attributed to the vote of Health.

(2) The main divisions, and the purposes, of the appropriation to the vote of Health, envisaged in subsection (1), are set out in the Schedule.

(3) The Appropriation Act, 2025 (Act No. 3 of 2025), applies to the appropriation envisaged in subsection (1), with the necessary changes.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Special Appropriation Act, 2026.
