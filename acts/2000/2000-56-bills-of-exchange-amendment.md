#### 46. Amendment of section 98 of Act 34 of 1964

46\. Section 98 of the principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection ( 1 ) of the following subsection:

“(1 ) If a dishonored bill or note is authorized [or required] to be protested, and the services of a notary cannot be obtained at the place where the bill or note is dishonored, any landowner or householder of the place may, in the presence of two witnesses, give a certificate, signed by them, attesting the dishonour of the bill, and such certificate shall in all respects operate as if it were a formal protest of the bill.”.
