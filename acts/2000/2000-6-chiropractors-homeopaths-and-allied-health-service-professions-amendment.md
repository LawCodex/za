# Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act 6 of 2000

ACT

To amend the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act, 1982, in order to extend the terms of oliice of the members of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Interim Council, retrospectively; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, asfollows:— Amendment of section 3 of Act 63 of 1982, as amended by section 4 of Act 40 of 1995 and section 1 of Act 91 of 1997

1\. Section 3 of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act, 1982, is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (e) of the following paragraph:

“(e) to make recommendations to the Minister on the constitution of the new council within a period not exceeding [36] 60 months calculated as from the date of commencement of the Chiropractor~Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act, 1995 (Act No. 40 of 1995); and”. Amendment of section 5 of Act 63 of 1982, as substituted by section 6 of Act 40 of 1995 and amended by section 2 of Act 91 of 1997

2\. Section 5 of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act, 1982, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (6) of the following subsection:

“(6) Subject to the provisions of section 6, the members of the council shall hold office as from the date contemplated in subsection (5) until the date of the constitution of a new council but not exceeding a period of [36] 60 months.”.—

#### 3. Short title ~o

3\. This Act is called the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act, 2000, and must be regarded as having come into operation on 13 February 1999.
