# Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Amendment Act 20 of 2009

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words in bo]d type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented tu 26 November 2009.)

ACT

To amend the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act, 2005, so as to substitute a date; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

parliament of the Republic of South Africa enacts as follows:- Amendment of section 1 of Act 28 of 2005, as amended by section 1 of Act 8 of 2006, section I of Act 13 of 2007 and section 1 of Act 7 of 2008 I. Section I of the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act, 2005 [No. 28 of 2005], is hereby amended by the substitution in subsection (3) for paragraph (a) of the following paragraph:

"(a) [30 December 2009] 30 December 2010: or".

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act is called the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Amendment Act, 2009, and comes into operation on 29 December 2009.
