# Close Corporations Amendment Act 25 of 2005

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 8 January 2006.)

ACT

To amend the Close Corporations Act, 1984, so as to empower a court to make an appropriate order in respect of a corporation and members of such corporation after the restoration by the registrar of the registration of that corporation; to adjust the requirements for membership of a corporation; and to allow a corporation to appoint another corporation as accounting officer; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
