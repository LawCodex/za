# South African Abattoir Corporation Act Repeal Act 17 of 2005

ACT

To repeal the South African Abattoir Corporation Act, 1992; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 1. EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as B follows:- Repeal of law

1\. The South African Abattoir Corporation Act, 1992 (Act No. 120 of 1992), is hereby repealed. 5

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the South African Abattoir Corporation Act Repeal Act, 2005.
