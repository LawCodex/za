# General Law Amendment Act 62 of 1955

ACT

To amend the Better Administration of Justice Act, 1896 ofthe Cape of Good Hope, the Better Administration of Justice and Remission of Treason Penalties Act, 1906 of the Cape of Good Hope, the South Africa Act, 1909, the Appellate · Division Further Jurisdiction Act, 1911, the Administration of Justice Act, 1912, the Administration of Estates Act, 1913, the South-West Africa Affairs Act, 1922, the Licences Consolidation Act, 1925, the Immorality Act, 1927, the Liquor Act, 1928, the Performing Animals Protection Act, 1935, the Insolvency Act, 1936, the Prescription Act, 1943, the Magistrates' Courts Act, 1944, the General Law Amend ment Act, 1949, the Prevention oflllegal Squatting Act, 1951, the Public Safety Act, 1953, and the law relating to the admission of advocates, to the alienation or mortgage of property, to pro'cedure in civil cases, to the possession and acquisition of stolen property, and to gambling houses.
