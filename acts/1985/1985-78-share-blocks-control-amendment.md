# Share Blocks Control Amendment Act 78 of 1985

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Insertion of sec· tions llA and llB in Act 59 of 1980.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Share Blocks Control Act, 1980, so as to provide for the furnishing by share block companies of information regarding share block schemes to certain les sees; and to regulate the sale of shares conferring a right to or an interest in the use of immovable property occupied by certain lessees; and to provide for matters connected there with.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 19June 1985.)
