# Estate Agents Amendment Act 10 of 1985

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Amendment of section 1 of Act 112 of 1976, as amended by section 1 of Act 60 of 1978, section 1 of Act 57 of 1980, section 1 of Act 53 of 1982 and section 1 of Act 51 of 1984.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Estate Agents Act, 1976, so as to make the provis· ions of the said Act applicable to close corporations doing business as estate agents; and to include the sale of interests in close corporations in the business of estate agents; and to provide for incidental matters.
