# Mental Health Amendment Act 16 of 1985

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Substitution of section 71 of Act 18 of 1973.

Short title.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Mental Health Act, 1973, so as to authorize the Minister to make contributions in respect of the maintenance of, and expenditure incurred in connection with the care and treatment of, certain patients.
