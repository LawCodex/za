# Attorneys Amendment Act 80 of 1985

ACT

To amend the Attorneys Act, 1979, so as to replace an obsolete expression; to further regulate the investment of money in and payments from the Attorneys, Notaries and Convey ancers Fidelity Guarantee Fund; to make further provision with regard to the payment of grants and honoraria from the said fund; and to increase the maximum fines which may be imposed by the council of a law society; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
