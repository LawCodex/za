ACT

To amend the Prisons Act, 1959, so as to extend the definitions of

"photograph" and "take" ·and to define the word "pub lish"; to make provision for the temporary promotion of commissioned officers; to extend the power of the Minister to enter into agreements with other governments relating to the reception and detention of certain prisoners; and to extend the power of the Commissioner to delegate certain powers delegated to him; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 12 March 1985.)
