# Indians Education Amendment Act 64 of 1985

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Amendment of section 3A of Act 6i of1965, as inserted by section 3 of Act 78 of 1984.

Short title.

ACT

To amend the Indians Education Act, 1965, so as to further regu late the power of college councils relating to statutes; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 6/une 1985.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the House of Delegates of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section 3A of the Indians Education Act, 1965, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (2) of the following subsection:

"(2) (a) A college council [established for a college of edu cation] may [name statutes] frame-

(i) a statute providing for the general government of the college of education or technical college con cerned in relation to any matter not specifically prescribed by any law;~

(ii) regulations for the better carrying out of such sta tute:

Provided that- ( aa) a statute or regulation in so far as it relates to the composition of a college senate of a college of edu cation shall not be framed, amended, added to or repealed except after consultation with such col lege senate;

(bb) a statute or regulation in so far as it relates to the studies, instruction, examinations or discipline of students at a college of education shall not be framed, amended, added to or repealed except after consultation with the college senate of such college of education. 25

(b) [No statute framed under paragraph (a) shall be in force unless it is approved by the Minister and is published in the Gazette] Any statute or regulation which the college council proposes to frame under paragraph (a) shall be submitted to the Minister for his approval and, if ap- 30 proved by him, shall be published in the Gazette and shall have force and effect on such publication.".

2\. This Act shall be called the Indians Education Amendment Act, 1985.
