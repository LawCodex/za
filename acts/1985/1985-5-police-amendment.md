# Police Amendment Act 5 of 1985

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ Amendment of section 27 A of Act 7 of 1958, as inserted by section 8 of Act 90 of 1977.

Short title.

] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to make further provision in relation to the prohibition of the making of sketches and the taking of photographs of certain persons and the publication thereof.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 12 March 1985.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section 27A of the Police Act, 1958, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (2) of the following subsection:

"(2) For the purposes of this section- 5 'photograph' includes any [cinematograph film, any picture intended for exhibition through the medium of a mechanical device, and any film cassette, mag netic tape or video-plate] picture, visually percept ible image, depiction or any other similar rep- 10 resentation of the person concerned;

'take', in relation to a photograph, includes to per form any act which by itself or as part of a process or as one of a sequence of acts renders possible the production of a photograph;

'publish', in relation to a photograph, includes to ex hi bit, show, televise, represent or reproduce.".

2\. This Act shall be called the Police Amendment Act, 1985.
