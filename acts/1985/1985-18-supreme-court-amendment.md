ACT

To amend the Supreme Court Act, 1959, so as to provide, in con sequence of the provisions of the new Constitution, for the submission of a certain address to the State President by each of the respective Houses of Parliament; to increase cer tain fines; to increase the extent of the exemptions relating to those classes of property which are to a limited extent exempt from execution; and to substitute a certain official title; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 15 . March 1985.)
