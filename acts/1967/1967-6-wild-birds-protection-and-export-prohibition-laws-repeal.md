# Wild Birds Protection and Export Prohibition Laws Repeal Act 6 of 1967

ACT

J o repeal the Wild Birds Protection Act, 1934, and the Wild Birds Export Prohibition Act, 1959.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 9th February, 1967.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Asembly of the Republic of South Mrica, as follows:-

1\. The Wild Birds Protection Act, 1934, and the Wild Birds Export Prohibition Act, 1959, are hereby repealed.

2\. This Act shall be called the Wild Birds Protection and Export Prohibition Laws Repeal Act, 1967.
