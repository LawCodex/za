# Merchandise Marks Amendment Act 55 of 1967

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Merchandise Marks Act, 1941, relating to the unauthorized use of certain emblems, the prohibition relating to the sale of bottles marked with the owner's name and the application of the said Act.
