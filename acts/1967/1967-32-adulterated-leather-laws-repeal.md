# Adulterated Leather Laws Repeal Act 32 of 1967

ACT

To repeal the Adulterated Leather Act, 1958.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 13th March, 1967.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. The Adulterated Leather Act, 1958, is hereby repealed.

2\. This Act shall be called the Adulterated Leather Laws Repeal Act, 1967.
