# National Supplies Procurement Amendment Act 70 of 1976

ACT

To amend the National Supplies Procurement Act, 1970, in order to empower the Minister of Economic Affairs to regulate, control or prohibit in certain circumstances transport services or other services supplied by the State; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.
