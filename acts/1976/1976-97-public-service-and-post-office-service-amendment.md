# Public Service and Post Office Service Amendment Act 97 of 1976

ACT

To amend the Public Service Act, 1957, and the Post Office Service Act, 1974, so as to further regulate the requirements in regard to citizenship that apply in the case of permanent appointments in the public service and the service of the Department of Posts and Telecommunications, respectively.
