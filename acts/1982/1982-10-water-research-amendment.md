# Water Research Amendment Act 10 of 1982

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Water Research Act, 1971, so as to authorize the Minister of Environment Affairs to . write off from time to time certain irrecoverable rates, charges and interest; and to provide for the delegation of powers by the said Minister;
