# Police Amendment Act 45 of 1982

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to empower the Minister of Police to confer a temporary higher rank on a commissioned officer; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 4 March 1982.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. The following section is hereby inserted in the Police Act, Insertion of 1958, after section 3A: section 3B in Act 7 of 1958.

5 "Conferring of temporary higher ranks on commision ed officers.

3B. (1) The Minister may from time to time tem porarily promote in rank any commissioned officer, and the officer concerned shall hold the temporary higher rank so conferred on him during the pleasure of the Minister.

(2) The fact that a commissioned officer holds a temporary rank under subsection (1) shall not dero gate from the powers of the State President under section 3 in respect ofthat officer.".

2\. This Act shall be called the Police Amendment Act, 1982. Short title.
