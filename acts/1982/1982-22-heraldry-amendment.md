# Heraldry Amendment Act 22 of 1982

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Heraldry Act, 1962, so as to provide that certain acts in respect of the coat of arms of the Republic shall be punishable; and to provide that the furnishing of any representation purporting to be a true representation of a family coat of arms shall be punishable unless a certain certificate has been issued in respect l)f such representation; to amend the Admission of Persons to the Republic Regulation Act, 1972, so as to authorize removal from the Republic on the ground of a contravention of section 22A of the Heraldry Act, 1962; and to make provision for matters incidental thereto.
