# Share Blocks Amendment Act 33 of 1982

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Share Blocks Control Act, 1980, so as to restrict the receipt of any consideration in respect of shares in any company which is to be formed for the purpose of operating a share block scheme; and to provide for incidental matters.
