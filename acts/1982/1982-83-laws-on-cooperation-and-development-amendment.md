# Laws on Cooperation and Development Amendment Act 83 of 1982

ACT

To amend the Black Affairs Act, 1959, so as to authorize the State President to make regulations providing for the preser vation of secrecy in connection with matters dealt with by the Commission for Co-operation and Development; and to pro vide for penalties for contraventions of such regulations; to amend the Community Councils Act, 1977, so as to authorize community councils to borrow money and to apply or invest money of such councils not immediately required for the purposes of such councils; and to provide for matters con nected therewith.
