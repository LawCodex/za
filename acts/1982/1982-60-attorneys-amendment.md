# Attorneys Amendment Act 60 of 1982

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ J Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments. Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Attorneys Act, 1979, so as to further regulate exemption from service under articles; to further regulate the admission of attorneys practising in certain countries or territories; to extend the purpose for which the Attorneys, Notaries and Conveyancers Fidelity Guarantee Fund may be applied; and to further extend the prohibition on the can vassing of certain types of work; and to provide for inciden tal matters.
