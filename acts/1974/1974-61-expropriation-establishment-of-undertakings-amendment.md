# Expropriation (Establishment of Undertakings) Amendment Act 61 of 1974

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Expropriation (Establishment of Undertakings) Act, 1951, relating to definitions; and to the powers of expropriation of certain persons; and to provide that certain persons may take the right to use land tempo rarily; and for matters connected therewith.
