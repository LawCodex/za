# Limitation and Disclosure of Finance Charges Amendment Act 62 of 1974

ACT

To amend the Limitation and Disclosure of Finance Charges Act, 1968, so as to define certain expressions; to make provision for prescribing maximum finance charge rates by regulation;

and to exempt certain institutions and transactions from certain provisions of tbe said Act; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
