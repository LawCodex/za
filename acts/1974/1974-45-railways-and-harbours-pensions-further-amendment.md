# Railways and Harbours Pensions Further Amendment Act 45 of 1974

ACT

To substitute section 1 of the Railways and Harbours Pensions Amendment Act, 1941, so as to provide for new definitions;

to amend sections 2, 4 and 5, to substitute sections 3, 7 and 8, and to repeal section 6 of the said Act so as to amend the conditions with regard to the payment of pension benefits, to simplify the calculation thereof and to eliminate cumbersome procedures; to insert a new section SA in the said Act so as to apply the provisions thereof to South West Africa; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.
