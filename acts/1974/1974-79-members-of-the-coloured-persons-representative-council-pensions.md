# Members of the Coloured Persons Representative Council Pensions Act 79 of 1974

ACT

To provide for the payment of pensions and other benefits to members of the Coloured Persons Representative Council of the Republic of South Africa, and to their widows and children; and for incidental matters.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 11 November 1974.)
