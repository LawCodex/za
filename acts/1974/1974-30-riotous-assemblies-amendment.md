# Riotous Assemblies Amendment Act 30 of 1974

ACT

To amend the Riotous Assemblies Act, 1956, so as to delete the definitions of certain expressions and to define or further define certain expressions; to further regulate the power to prohibit gatherings in certain instances and the manner of publication of such a prohibition; to amend certain penalty clanses; to transfer the power or a magistrate to close places to certain police officers; and to substitute the words "State President" and "Republic" for the words "Governor General" and "Union"; and to provide for incidental matters.
