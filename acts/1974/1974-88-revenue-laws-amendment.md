# Revenue Laws Amendment Act 88 of 1974

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Marketable Securities Tax Act, 1948, with regard to the definition of "joint account", so as to effect certain textual changes in section 3 and to provide for a further exemption from marketable securities tax; to amend the provisions of the Transfer Duty Act, 1949, relating to the rates of transfer duty and the officials to whom transfer duty is required to be paid, so as to effect certain textual changes in section 9 and to provide for the utilization of certain declarations for the purpose of acknowledging, by means of a machine, the receipt of transfer duty; to amend the provisions of the Stamp Duties Act, 1968, so as to provide for further exemptions from stamp duties, to effect certain textual changes in section 23, to provide for an alternative method for the payment of stamp duty on certain classes of policies of insurance, to declare certain activities in con nection with adhesive stamps to be offences, to abolish certain stamp duties, to change the rates of stamp duty payable in respect of certain bonds, to effect certain textual changes in Item 15 of Schedule 1 to the said Act and to aboHsh certain fees of office; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to II November 1974.)
