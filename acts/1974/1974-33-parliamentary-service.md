# Parliamentary Service Act 33 of 1974

ACT

To give effect to a resolution of the Senate and of the House of Assembly to establish a staff of Parliament; for that purpose to amend the Electoral Consolidation Act, 1946, the Republic of South Africa Constitution Act, 1961, the Payment of Members of Parliament Act, 1961, the Justices of the Peace and Commissioners of Oaths Act, 1963, the Powers and Privileges of Parliament Act, 1963, the Customs and Excise Act, 1964, and the Parliamentary Service and Adminis trators' Pensions Act, 1971; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 27 August 1974.)
