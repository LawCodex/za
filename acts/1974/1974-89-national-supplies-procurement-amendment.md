# National Supplies Procurement Amendment Act 89 of 1974

ACT

To amend the provisions of the National Supplies Procurement Act, 1970, relating to definitions; so as to further regulate the appointment by the Minister of controllers, officials and in spectors, the constitution by him of bodies, and the powers, duties and functions of such persons; and so as to provide for the granting of exemptions; for the disposal of certain moneys; and for incidental matters.
