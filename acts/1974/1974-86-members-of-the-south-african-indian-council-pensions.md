# Members of the South African Indian Council Pensions Act 86 of 1974

ACT

To provide for the payment of pensions and other benefits to members of the South African Indian Council and to their widows and children; and for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 11 November 1974.)
