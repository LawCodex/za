# Police Amendment Act 68 of 1974

ACT

To amend the Police Act, 1958, so as to make further provision for the giving of notice of certain actions and the causes thereof; and to regulate the payment of the salaries or wages and allowances of missing members of the Force and other employees of the South African Police; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
