# Environment Conservation Amendment Act 52 of 1994

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments ..

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments ..

ACT

To amend the Environment Conservation Act, 1989, so as to adjust certain definitions; to extend the Minister's powers with regard to the determination of policy; to make provision for the appointment of additional members to the Committee for Environmental Co-ordination; to substitute references to obsolete provisions and names; to empower the Minister to make regulations relating to the application and enforcement of international conventions, treaties and agreements; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
