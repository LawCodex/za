# Forestry Laws Rationalisation and Amendment Act 51 of 1994

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To provide for the rationalisation of certain laws relating to forestry matters that remained in force in various areas of the national territory of the Republic by virtue of section 229 of the Constitution; to amend the Wattle Bark Industry Act, 1960, so as to redefine certain expressions; to amend the Forest Act, 1984, so as to redefine certain expressions; and to delete certain obsolete provisions; to amend the Management of State Forests Act, 1992, so as to redefine a certain expression; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
