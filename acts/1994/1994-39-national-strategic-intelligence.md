# National Strategic Intelligence Act 39 of 1994

ACT

To define the functions of members of the National Intelligence Structures; to establish a National Intelligence Co-ordinating Committee and to define its functions in respect of intelligence relating to the security of the Republic; and to provide for the appointment of a Co-ordinator for Intelligence as chairperson of the National Intelligence Co-ordinating Committee, and to define his or her functions; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

15 Regulations

6\. The President may make regulations as to any matter which is necessary or expedient to be prescribed in order that the purpose of this Act may be achieved.

#### 7. Repeal of laws

7\. The Security Intelligence and State Security Council Act, 1972 (Act No. 64 20 of 1972), is hereby repealed.

#### 8. Short title and commencement

8\. This Act shall be called the National Strategic Intelligence Act, 1994, and shall come into operation on a date fixed by the President by proclamation in the Gazette.
