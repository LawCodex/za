2\. The powers and duties in respect of the issue of and control over South African passports and travel documents which immediately prior to 27 April 1994 were vested in the State President of the Republic of South Africa under 20 the provisions of the Republic of South Africa Constitution Act, 1983 (Act No. 110 of 1983), shall, subject to the provisions of this Act, vest in the Government of the Republic of South Africa with effect from 27 April 1994.

#### 3. South African citizen entitled to South African passport

3\. Subject to the provisions of this Act, every South African citizen shall be 25 entitled to a South African passport.

#### 4. Regulations

4\. (1) The Minister may make regulations regarding-

(a) the manner in which application is to be made for the issue of a South African passport or travel document, or for the renewal thereof, 30 including-

(i) any particulars or information which is to be furnished in the application;

(ii) any documents which are to accompany any such application or are to be produced when making any such application; and 35 (iii) any forms which are to be used in any such application;

(b) endorsements on South African passports and travel documents, periods of validity and other restrictions;

(c) the circumstances under which the issue of a South African passport or travel document may be refused or such passport or travel document 40 may be withdrawn;

(2) Any regulation made under subsection (1) may provide that any person who contravenes a provision thereof or fails to comply therewith shall be guilty of an offence and on conviction be liable to a fine, or to imprisonment for a 15 period not exceeding five years.

#### 5. Saving

5\. Anything done, whether under a law or otherwise, prior to the commence ment of this Act, and which can be done under a provision of this Act, shall be deemed to have been done under this Act.

20 Short title

6\. This Act shall be called the South African Passports and Travel Documents Act, 1994.
