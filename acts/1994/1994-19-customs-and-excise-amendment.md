#### 15. Repeal of laws, and savings ·

15\. (1) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2); the laws mentioned in the 35 second column of Schedule 2 are hereby repealed in respect of the various areas of the national territory of the Republic as. set out in the fourth column of Schedule 2 to the extent that such laws were in force in such areas.

(2) Anything done under a provision repealed by subsection (1) and which is capable of being done in terms of a provision of the principal Act shall be 40 deemed to have been done in terms of the latter provision.

#### 16. Short title

16\. This Act shall be called the Customs and Excise Amendment Act, 1994. CUSTOMS AND EXCISE AMENDMENT ACT, 1994 . Act No. 19, 1994
