# Integration of Labour Laws Act 49 of 1994

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:·

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments~ · Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To provide for the repeal of the labour laws mentioned in Schedule 1; the extension of the labour laws mentioned in Schedule 2 to the whole of the national territory of the Republic referred to in section 1 of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1993 (Act No. 200 of 1993); and to provide for powers of regulation to regulate transitional matters.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 23 November 1994.) B EIT ENACfED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

Repeal of laws  
1\. Subject to sections 3 and 4, the laws mentioned in Schedule 1 are repealed.  
5 Extension of labour laws  
2\. Subject to sections 3 and4, the laws mentioned in Schedule 2 shall apply in  
the whole of the national territory referred to in section 1 of the Constitution of  
the ~epublic of South Africa, 1993 (Act No. 200 of 1993).  
Regulations  
10 3. The Minister may make regulations regulating any matter arising from the  
repeal of the laws referred to in section 1 or the application of the laws referred  
to in section 2 in those parts of the national territory in which they did not apply  
prior to the commencement of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa,  
1993 (Act No. 200 of 1993), including, but not limited to-  
15 (a) the transfer of assets, liabilities, rights, obligations and funds;  
(b) the status of trade unions, employers' organisations and industrial  
councils and other statutory bodies registered or established in terms of  
the laws referred to in Schedule 1 and Schedule 2;  
(c) the status of collective agreements, arbitration awards and industrial  
20 court decisions binding in terms of the laws referred to in Schedule 1  
and Schedule 2;  
(d) the provision for the resolution of disputes and claims that arise before  
the repeal of the laws in Schedule 1;

Commencement  
4\. (1) This Act shall come into operation on a dateto be determined by the  
President by proclamation in the Gazette.  
10 (2) Different dates may be so determined for the coming into operation of  
section 1 with respect to each of the laws mentioned in Schedule 1.  
(3) Different dates may be so determined for the coming into operation of  
section 2 in different areas with respect to each of the laws mentioned in  
Schedule 2.  
15 Short title  
5\. This Act shall be called the Integration of Labour Laws Act, 1994.
