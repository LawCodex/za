3\. The money of the fund shall be utilised- ( a) to finance reconstruction and development projects and programmes identified by the Minister responsible for the co-ordinated implemen- 25 tation thereof; and (b) for the defraying of costs incidental to the administration of this Act.

#### 8. Investment of money not immediately required

8\. (1) Any money of the fund which is not required for immediate use may be invested with the Public Investment Commissioners or with a financial institu- 20 tion approved by the Minister and may be withdrawn when required.

(2) Any unexpended balance of the money of the fund at the end of . any financial year shall be carried forward as a credit of the fund to the next succeeding financial year.
