# Admission of Advocates Amendment Act 55 of 1994

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Admission of Advocates Act, 1964, so as to abolish the requirement that must be complied with by persons in respect of the Latin language in order to be admitted to practise as advocates; and to delete or substitute certain obsolete words and expressions; and to amend laws of the former Republics of Transkei, Bophuthatswana and Venda with regard to the admission of advocates; and to provide for matters connected therewith. · · (English text signed by the President.)
