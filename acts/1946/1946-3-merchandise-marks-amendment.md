# Merchandise Marks Amendment Act 3 of 1946

ACT

To amend the Merchandise Marks Act, 1941.

' . . .

(Afrikaans Text signed by the Governor~General.) (Assented to on 28th March, 1946,) .B EIT ENACTED by the King's Mo11t Excellent Majesty, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Union of South Africa, as follows :

1\. Section ten of the Merchandise Marks Act, 1941, is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (c) of sub-section (I) of the following paragraph :-

"(c) there sh.all be applied to them in a conspicuous manner and as specified in the notice, words stating clearly the materials of which they are composed and the percentages of such materials calculated either by weight or by volume as prescribed in the notice."

2\. This Act shall be called the Merchandise Marks Amend ment Act, 1946.
