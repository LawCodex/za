# Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Amendment Act 8 of 2006

ACT

To amend the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act, 2005, so as to substitute a date; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

#### 1. BE IT ENACTED hy the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 1 of Act 28 of 2005

1\. Section 1 of the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Act, 2005 is hereby amended by the suhstitution in subsections (21, (3), (4), (5) and (6) for the date “31 July 2006”, wherever it occurs, of the date “30 September 2007”.

#### 2. Short title and commencement

2\. This Act is called the Repeal of the Black Administration Act and Amendment of Certain Laws Amendment Act, 2006, and comes into operation on 30 July 2006. 10
