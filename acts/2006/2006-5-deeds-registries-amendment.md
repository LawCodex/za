# Deeds Registries Amendment Act 5 of 2006

ACT

To amend the Deeds Registries Act, 1937, so as to provide for the continued registration of registrable transactions concerning rights acquired in terms of or under the Black Administration Act, 1927, or proclamations, regulations or bylaws under the latter Act despite its repeal; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
