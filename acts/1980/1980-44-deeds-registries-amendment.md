# Deeds Registries Amendment Act 44 of 1980

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE: · I J Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments. Words underlined. with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To aniend the Deeds Registries Act, 1937, so as to provide for the registration of notarial variations and renewals of notarial prospecting · contracts; and to facilitate under certain circumstances the obtaining, by a joint holder of a certificate or deed of cession of rights to minerals, of a certificate of rights to minerals in respect of his share in such rights; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
