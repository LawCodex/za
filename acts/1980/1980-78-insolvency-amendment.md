# Insolvency Amendment Act 78 of 1980

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ J Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments. Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Insolvency Act, 1936, so as to provide that certain costs in connection with the engagement of a legal practitioner for the interrogation of a person at a meeting of creditors, shall be included in the costs of the sequestration of the estate; to amend the provisions which require that notice be given of the taxation by the Master of certain costs; and to delete the provisions which confer the power upon certain creditors and the insolvent to be present at such taxations and to object to certain costs;

and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 12 June 1980.)
