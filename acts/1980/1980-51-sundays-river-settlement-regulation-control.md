# Sundays River Settlement Regulation Control Act 51 of 1980

ACT

To provide for the assets, rights, liabilities and obligations of the Sundays River Settlement Management Board to be vested in the Division of Winterhoek; and for a special account to be kept by the council of the said Division and to be credited with certain moneys; to determine the purposes for which the moneys in the special account shall be utilized; to repeal the Sundays River Settlement Management Act, 1935; and to provide for incidental matterS.
