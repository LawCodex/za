# Credit Agreements Amendment Act 53 of 1987

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Amendment of section 1 of Act 75 of 1980, as amended by section 1 of Act 9 of 1985.

Short title and commence ment.

ACT

To amend the Credit Agreements Act' ·t980, so as to further define certain expressions; and to provide for incidental mat ters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 9 September 1987.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- .

1\. Section 1 of the Credit Agreements Act, 1980, is hereby amende~ ··

(a) by the substitution for the definition of "initial pay- 5 ment" of the following definition:

"'initial payment' means the amount to be paid by the credit receiver [on the date] in the case of a credit agreement which is a credit transaction;";

(b) by the substitution for the definition of "initial rental" 10 of the following definition:

"'initial rental' means the amount to be paid by the credit receiver [on the date] in the case of a credit agreement which is a leasing transaction;"; and (c) by the substitution for the definition of "Minister" of the following definition:

'"Minister' means the Minister of [Trade and Industry]

Economic Affairs and Technology;".

2\. This Act shall be called the Credit Agreements Amend ment Act, 1987, and shall be deemed to have come into opera- 20 tion on 2 March 1981.
