ACT

To amend the Mental Health Act, 1973, so as to alter obsolete designations; to authorize the Minister of Justice to assign powers or duties entrusted to him in terms of the said Act;

and to grant leave to President's patients to be absent from institutions or places where they are being detained; and to provide for incidental matters.
