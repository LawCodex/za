# Law of Evidence and the Criminal Procedure Act Amendment Act 103 of 1987

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Evidence of sexual intercourse by boy under 14 years may be adduced .

Amendment of section 153 of Act 51 of 1977.

Amendment of section 154 of Act 51 of 1977, as amended by section 12 of Act 33 of 1986.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To make provision that any accountable boy under the age of 14 years may be held legally responsible for any act or offence of which sexual intercourse with a female is an element; to amend the Criminal Procedure Act, 1977, so as to further regulate the circumstances in which certain criminal pro ceedings shall not take place in open court; and to extend the prohibition of the publication of certain information relating to certain offences; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.
