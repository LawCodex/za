ACT

To amend the Unemployment Insurance Act, 1966, with regard to the definition or "contributor"; in order to delete the pro vision in terms of which a contributor shall not be regarded as unemployed during any period in which he is undergoing certain training; to make different provision relating to the payment of maternity benefits; to make provision for the · payment of adoption benefits to. female contributors who adopt children; and to do away with a limitation which ap plies in respect of certain payments to the widowers of de ceased contributors; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
