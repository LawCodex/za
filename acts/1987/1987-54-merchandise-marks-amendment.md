# Merchandise Marks Amendment Act 54 of 1987

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ Amendment of section 1 of Act 17 of 1941, as amended by section 1 of Act 39 of 1952.

Substitution of section 14 of Act 17 of 1941, as amended by section 2 of Act 39 of 1952 and section 1 of Act 55 of 1967.

] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Merchandise Marks Act, 1941, so as to substitute the definition of "Minister"; to abolish certain acts which constitute offences; and to substitute or delete certain obso lete references therein; and to make provision for matters connected therewith.
