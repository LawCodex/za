# Universities Amendment Act 24 of 1968

ACT

To amend sections 15, 22, 25 and 27 of the Universities Act, 1955, in order to accord representation to the Department of Indian Affairs on the Joint Matriculation Board, to em power the Minister of National Education, in consultation with the Minister of Finance, to waive certain preferences and to grant subsidies to universities, and to provide for action on failure of the council of any university to comply ·with any condition subject to which a subsidy is paid; and to provide for other incidental matters.
