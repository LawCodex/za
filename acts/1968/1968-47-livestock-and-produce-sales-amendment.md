# Livestock and Produce Sales Amendment Act 47 of 1968

ACT

To amend the Livestock and Produce Sales Act, 1956, in order to exclude from the provisions of the said Act the business transacted by an auctioneer, agent or factor in certain cases; and to provide for the designation of officers to determine the volume of the business of an auctioneer, agent or factor for purposes of giving security and for matters incidental thereto.
