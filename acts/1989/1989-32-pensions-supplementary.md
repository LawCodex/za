# Pensions (Supplementary) Act 32 of 1989

ACT

To provide for the granting of certain pension benefits.

(Afrikaans text signed by the acting State President.) (Assented to 14 March 1989.) B EIT ENACfED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Granting of certain pension benefits

1\. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any law, the persons mentioned in the Schedule shall be entitled to the benefits contemplated therein. 5

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Pensions (Supplementary) Act, 1989.

## SCHEDULE

1\. The pension payable to Jacobus Cloete, formerly a sergeant in the South African Police, by virtue of section 10 of the General Pensions Act, 1979 (Act No. 10 29 of 1979), shall, as a special case, be increased to R6 000 per annum with effect from 1 September 1988.

2\. The pension payable in terms of the Government Service Pension Act, 1973 (Act No. 57 of 1973), to Johanna M. Rudman, formerly a housekeeper in the service of the provincial hospital, Port Elizabeth, shall be increased by an amount of R2 736 15 per annum with effect from 1 September 1988.
