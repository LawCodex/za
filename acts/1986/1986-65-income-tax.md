# Income Tax Act 65 of 1986

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To fix the rates of normal tax payable by persons other than com panies in respect of taxable incomes for the years of assess ment ending on 28 February 1987 and 30 June 1987, and by companies in respect of taxable incomes for years of assess ment ending during the period of 12 months ending on 31 March 1987; to amend the Income Tax Act, 1962; to amend the Income Tax Act, 1984; to amend the Income Tax Act, 1985; and to provide for incidental matters.
