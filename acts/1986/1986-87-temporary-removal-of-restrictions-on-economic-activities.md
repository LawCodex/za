# Temporary Removal of Restrictions on Economic Activities Act 87 of 1986

ACT

To empower the State President to suspend temporarily laws or conditions, limitations or obligations thereunder with regard to certain undertakings, industries, trades and occupations or to grant temporary exemption from the provisions there . of, if in his opinion circumstances exist under which the application of or compliance with those laws, conditions, lim itations or obligations unduly impedes the economic progress of the persons engaged in those undertakings, industries, trades and occupations, or competition in the fields in ques tion, or the creation of job opportunities; and to provide for incidental matters.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 3 September 1986.)
