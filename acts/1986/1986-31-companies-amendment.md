# Companies Amendment Act 31 of 1986

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Companies Act, 1973, so as to abolish annual duty payable by companies; to do away with the duty of compan ies to lodge annual returns; to increase the fees for the regis tration of a company and for the alteration of share capital and shares by a company; and to extend the duties of the au ditor of a company; and to provide for incidental matters.
