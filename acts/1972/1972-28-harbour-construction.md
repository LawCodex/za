ACT

To provide for the construction and equipment of a certain commercial harbour and for matters incidental thereto.

(2) The cost of construction and equipment of the commercial harbour authorized by subsection (1) shall be defrayed out of a loan raised by the State President under the authority oflaw and appropriated for that purpose by Parliament, or out of any other moneys so appropriated.

(3) The powers conferred by this section shall include the power to construct and equip the entrance works, entrance channel and all navigational channels, turning basins, wharves, docks, basins, jetties, piers, flood control systems, tide control systems, signal stations, navigational aids, yards, railway lines, sidings, buildings and any other facilities or appurtenances or additional works necessary for or incidental to the proper working of the said harbour.
