# Sea-Shore Amendment Act 38 of 1972

ACT

To amend the Sea-shore Act, 1935, so as to replace or further define certain expressions; to provide for increased penalties for contravention of the regulations; and to provide for additional delegation of powers by the Minister; to amend the Reservation of Separate Amenities Act, 1953, so as to regulate supervision of and control over certain parts of the sea and the sea-shore; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 16th May, 1972.)
