(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 30th March, 1972.) WHEREAS by deed bearing date the seventeenth day of Preamble. December, 1861, a joint stock company was formed in Grahamstown, in the Colony of the Cape of Good Hope, under the style of the Eastern Province Guardian Loan and Investment Company;

AND WHEREAS the company was incorporated under and by virtue of Act No. 20 of 1863 (Cape of Good Hope);

AND WHEREAS the company's objects and powers were increased by the Eastern Province Guardian Loan and Invest ment Company Amendment, Indemnity and Further Powers (Private) Act, 1920 (Act No. 25 of 1920);

AND WHEREAS the company has become a wholly owned subsidiary of The Syfret and South African Trust Companies Limited;

AND WHEREAS it is expedient to alter the company's name to

"SYFRETS TRUST AND EXECUTOR COMPANY (GRAHAMSTOWN)" so as to indicate that it is a subsidiary of The Syfret and South African Trust Companies Limited;
