# Dissolution of Marriages on Presumption of Death Act 23 of 1979

ACT

To provide for the dissolution of marriages of persons · presumed to be dead. · (Afrikaans text signed by the Siaie President. ) · (Assented to 16 March 1979.) BE IT ENACfED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic · of South Africa, as follows:- ·. r ': '· ' • • • , ;

1\. Any provincial or local division of the Supreme Court of South Africa making an order that the death of any married person shall be presumed, may, when making that order or at any time thereafter, on the application of such person's spouse, make an order that the marriage in question shall be deemed to' have .been . dissolved , by death ,.as from · a date: determined . by· the court, . whereupon that marriage shall for all purposes be deemed to have been dissolved by death as from the date so determined.
