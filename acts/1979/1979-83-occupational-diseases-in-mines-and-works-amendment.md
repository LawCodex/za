# Occupational Diseases in Mines and Works Amendment Act 83 of 1979

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ Increase of benefits and special awards.

ACT

To amend the provisions of the Occupational Diseases in Mines and Works Act; 1973, so as to provide for the further increase of benefits and special awards; to make different provision regarding the restrictions relating to the performance of work subject to which certain certificates of fitness may be issued; and to provide for matters incidental thereto.
