# Protection of Businesses Amendment Act 114 of 1979

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ Amendment of section 1 of Act 99 of 1978.

Short title.

] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Protection of Businesses Act, 1978, so as to extend the restriction on the enforcement of certain foreign judgments, orders, directions, arbitration awards and letters of request. · (!tfrikaans text signed by the State President.) · . (!tssented to 2 July 1979.) · · · .. ·· •

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the . House of Assembly ·of the Republic of .. South Africa, as follows:- ·. · 1.· Section 1 of the Protection of Businesses Act, 1978, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (3) of the following subsection: . . .

"(3) In the application of subsection · (l) (a) an act or transaction shall be any act or transaction which took place at any time, whether before or after the commencement of this Act, and is connected with the production, importation, 10 · exportation, refinement, possession, use or sale of or ownership to any matter or material, of whatever nature, whether within, outside, into or from the Republic [by, on behalf of or of producers of such matter or material].''.

:2. This Act shall be called the Protection of Businesses Amendment Act, 1979. ' . .. '
