ACT

To fix the rates of normal tax payable by persons other than companies in respect of taxable incomes for the years of assessment ending on 28 February .1991 and 30 June 1991, and by companies In respect of taxable incomes for years of assessment ending during the period of 12 months endirig on 31 March 1991; to amend the Income Tax Act, 1962; to withdraw certain Government Notices; and to provide for incidental matters.
