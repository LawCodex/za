# Broadcasting Amendment Act 113 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square, brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Broadcasting Act, 1976, so as to replace the definition of "Minister" and delete the definition of "Republic"; to empower the Minister to postpone the payment of television licence fees and to grant exemption from the payment thereof and of penalties; to further regulate the closing date of the financial year of the SABC; and to replace the long title; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
