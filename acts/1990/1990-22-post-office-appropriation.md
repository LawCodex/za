# Post Office Appropriation Act 22 of 1990

ACT

To appropriate a sum of money for the requirements of the Department of Posts and Telecommunications in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 1991. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 23 March 1990.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- .

#### 1. Appropriation of moneys for requirements of Department

1\. Subject to the provisions of the Post Office Act, 1958 (Act No. 44' of 1958), 5 there are hereby .appropriated, as a charge to the Post Office Fund, for the requirements of the Department of Posts and Telecommunications in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 1991, the sums of money set forth in the Schedule.

#### 2. Purposes to which moneys shall be applied

2\. The moneys appropriated by section 1 shall be applied to the purposes set forth 10 in the Schedule and more particularly specified in Part II of the Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure of the Department of Posts and Telecommunications (R.P. 15-1990), as submitted to and approved by Parliament.
