# Inquests Amendment Act 45 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold. type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Inquests Act, 1959, so as to insert or replace certain definitions; to increase certain fines; to further regulate the holding of inquests; to provide that an inquest may also be held by a judge or regional magistrate; to make provision for a joint inquest in certain cases; and to rectify or delete obsolete expressions; and to . provide for matters ~onnected therewith.
