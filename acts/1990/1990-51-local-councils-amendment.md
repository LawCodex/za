# Local Councils Amendment Act 51 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Act No. 51, 1990 Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Local Councils Act (House of Assembly), 1987, so as to adjust the definition of Minister; and to authorize the Minister of the Budget and Local Government to assign certain functions to certain officers; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
