ACT

To provide for the joint exercise and performance on a regional basis of certain powers and duties in relation to certain functions by local bodies In the area comprising the Province of Natal and that part of KwaZulu which falls outside that Province; to that end 'to provide for the delimitation of regions; for the establishment · of joint services boards; and for the constitution, functioning, functions, powers, duties, assets, rights, employees and financing of such boards; to amend certain laws; and to provide for matters connected therewith. · · (English text signed by the State President.)
