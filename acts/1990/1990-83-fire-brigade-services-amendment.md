# Fire Brigade Services Amendment Act 83 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Fire Brigade Services Act, 1987, so as to insert, amend or replace certain definitions; to further regulate the functions of the Fire Brigade Board; to further regulate the establishment, maintenance and employment of services of local authorities; to further regulate the employment of designated services; to make provision for the appointment of an acting chief fire officer; to make provision for the institution of a fire brigade reserve force; to make provision for the institution and awarding of decorations, medals and service certificates; to authorize the Adminis trator to designate a certain category of persons; to enable the Minister to recover an amount spent in the handling of urgent cases; to further regulate the making of regulations; and to authorize a chief fire officer to issue a written instruction not only to the owner but also to the authorized agent of an owner; and to provide for incidental matters.
