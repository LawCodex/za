# Associated Health Service Professions Amendment Act 10 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ J Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Associated Health Service Professions Act, 1982, so as to amend the definition of "Minister"; to limit the general functions of the South African Associated Health Service Professions Board; and to delete the requirement that limited registration may not exceed five years; and to provide for incidental matters. (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 9 March 1990.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 1 of Act 63 of 1982, as amended by section 1 of Act 108 of 1985

1\. Section 1 of the Associated Health Service Professions Act, 1982 (hereinafter 5 referred to as the principal Act}, is hereby amended by the substitution for the definition of "Minister" of the following definition:

"'Minister' means the Minister of National Health and [Welfare] Population Development;".

Amendment of section 4 of Act 63 of 1982 10 . 2. Section 4 of the principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (g) of subsection (1) of the following paragraph:

"(g) conduct or arrange courses for the supplementary training of practitioners

[and students] and render financial assistance in respect of such courses;". Amendment of section 16C of Act 63 of 1982, as inserted by section 6 of Act 108 of 15 1985

3\. Section 16C of th~ principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution in paragraph (a) of subsection (2) for the words preceding subparagraph (i) of the following words:

"for such period [not exceeding five years] or periods as the board may 20 determine and before the expiration of which he shall satisfy the board that-".

#### 4. Short title

4\. This Act shall be called the Associated Health Service Professions Amendment Act, 1990.
