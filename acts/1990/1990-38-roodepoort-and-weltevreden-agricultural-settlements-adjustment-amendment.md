# Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Amendment Act 38 of 1990

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

ACT

To amend the Roodepoort and Weltevreden Agricultural Settlements Adjustment Act, 1966, so as to regulate the ownership of those portions of the common grazing ground of the Roodepoort Agricultural Settlement which were not divided into lots; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
