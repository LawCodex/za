# Post Office Second Amendment Act 53 of 1997

ACT

To amend the Post Office Act, 1958, so as to make other provision in connection with the membership of Telkom SA Limited; to effect textual improvements; and to allow the said Teikom to issue certain financial instruments for as long as the majority of its issued equity shares are held by the State; and to provide for incidental matters.
