# Abolition of Public Administration Commissions Act 48 of 1997

ACT

To abolish the Public Service Commission and the provincial service commissions concerned; and to provide for incidental matters.

(Afrikaans text signed by the President.) (Assented to 25 September 1997.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, asfollows:— Abolition of Public Service Commission and provincial service commissions concerned

1\. The Public Service Commission established by section 209(1) of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africrq 1993 (Act No. 200 of 1993), and the provincial service commissions established by the Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 1 of 1994 of Northern Province), the North West Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 2 of 1994 of North-West), the Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 3 of 1994 of Free State), the Northern Cape Provincial Sesvice Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 3 of 1994 of Northern Cape), the Provincial Service Commission Act (Eastern Transvaal), 1994 (Act No. 3 of 1994 of Mpumalanga), the Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 3 of 1994 of Gauteng), the Western Cape Provincial Service Commission Law, 1994 (Act No. 5 of 1994 of Western Cape), the Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Eastern Cape) (Act No. 5 of 1994 of Eastern Cape) and the KwaZulu-NTatal Provincial Service Commission Act, 1994 (Act No. 6 of 1994 of KwaZul~Tatd), are hereby abolished.

#### 2. Short titIe and commencement

2\. This Act shall be called the Abolition of Public Administration Commissions Act, 1997, and shall come into operation on a date fixed by the President by proclamation in the Gazette.
