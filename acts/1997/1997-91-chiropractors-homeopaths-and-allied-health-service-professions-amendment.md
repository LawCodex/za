# Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act 91 of 1997

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act, 1982, in order to extend the terms of office of members of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Interim Council; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(E?lgli.dl text signed by the President. ) (Assented to 26 November 1997. )

#### 1. BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa. asfollows:- Amendment of section 3 of Act 63 of 1982, as amended by section 4 of Act 40 of 1995

1\. Section 3 of the Chiropractors. Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act. 1982. is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (e) of the following paragraph:

“(e) to make recommendations to the Minister on the constitution of a new council within a period not exceeding [two years] 36 months calculated as from the date of commencement of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act. 1995; and”. 10 Amendment of section 5 of Act 63 of 1982, as substituted by section 6 of Act 40 of

2\. Section 5 of the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Act, 1982. is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (6) of the following subsection:

“(6) Subject to the provisions of section 6, the members of the council shall hold office as from the date contemplated in subsection (5) until the date of the constitution of a new council but not exceeding a period of [two years] 36 months.”.

#### 3. Short title

3\. This Act shall be called the Chiropractors, Homeopaths and Allied Health Service Professions Amendment Act, 1997.
