#### 2. Vacancies among permanent delegates

2\. (1) Within seven days after the occurrence of a vacancy among the permanent delegates to the National Council of Provinces, the Chairperson of the National Council of Provinces shall in writing inform the Speaker of the relevant provincial legislature of such vacancy. 15

(2) The Speaker concerned shall forthwith in writing call upon the party which nominated the vacating permanent delegate to nominate a person in his or her stead.

(3) After a person has been nominated in writing by the party concerned, the person shall be appointed as a permanent delegate by the provincial legislature- ( a) within 14 days of the date upon; which the nomination is received by the Speaker if that provincial legislature is then in session; or (b) if that provincial legislattire'is not irl sessfon when the nomination is received, within 14 days after the commencement of the next session.

(4) Immediately.after the appointment of a permanentdelegate in terms of subsection (3), the Speaker of the provincial legislature concerned shall inform the Chairperson of the National Council of Provinces of such appointment in writing.

#### 3. Short title

3\. This Act shall be called the National Council of Provinces (Permanent Delegates Vacancies) Act, 1997.
