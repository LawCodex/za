# Repeal of Local Government Laws Act 42 of 1997

ACT

To repeal certain laws pertaining to local government; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(Afrikaans text signed by the President.) (Assented to 25 September 1997.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa asfollows:—

#### 1. Repeal of laws

1\. The laws mentioned in the Schedule are hereby repealed to the extent set out in the third column of the Schedule. 5

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Repeal of Local Government Laws Act, 1997. —
