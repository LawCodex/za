# Telecommunications Amendment Act 12 of 1997

ACT

To amend the Telecommunications Act, 1996, so as to provide for the selection and transfer of personnel from the Department of Communications to the South African Telecommunications Regulatory Authority and the Universal Service Agency; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
