#### 2. Establishment

2\. (1) There is a Budget Council consisting of—

(a) the Ministeq and (b) the MEC for finance of each province.

(2) The Minister is the Chairperson of the Budget Council.

#### 3. Functions

3\. The Budget Council is a body in which the national government and the provincial governments consult on— 25

(u) any fiscal, budgetary or financial matter affecting the provincial sphere of government;

(b) any proposed legislation or policy which has a financial implication for the provinces, or for any specific province or provinces;

(c) any matter concerning the financial management, or the monitoring of the finances, of the provinces, or of any specific province or provinces; or (d) asty other matter which the Minister has referred to the Council. ### PART 2 — LOCAL GOVERNMENT BUDGET FORUM

#### 5. Establishment

5\. (1) There is a Local Government Budget Fosum consisting of—

(a) the Minister;

(b) the MEC for finance of each province;

(c) five representatives nominated by the national organisation recognised in terms of the Organised Local Government Act, 1997; and (d) one representative nominated by each provincial organisation recognised in terms of that Act.

(2) The Minister is the Chairperson of the Budget Forum.

#### 6. Functions

6\. The Budget Forum is a body in which the national government, the provincial governments and organised local government consult on—

(u) any fiscal, budgetary or financial matter affecting the local sphere of government;

(b) any proposed legislation or policy which has a financial implication for local government;

(c) any matter concerning the financial management, or the monitoring of the finances, of local government; or (d) any other matter which the Minister has referred to the Forum.

#### 7. Meetings

7\. (1) The Minister convenes the meetings of the Budget Forum, which must meet at least once in each financial year.

(2) hfeetings of the Budget Forum maybe attended by—

(a) the Chairperson of the Commission or a delegation of the Commission designated by the Chairperson of the Commission; and (b) any other persons by invitation.

#### 10. Division of Revenue Bill

10\. (1) Each year when the Annual Budget is introduced, the Minister must introduce in the National Assembly a Division of Revenue Bill for the financial year to which that Budget relates.

(2) The Division of Revenue Bill must specify— 20

(a) the share of each sphere of government of the revenue raised nationally for the relevant financial year;

11\. This Act is called the Intergovernmental Fiscal Relations Bill, 1997, and takes effect on 1 January 1998.
