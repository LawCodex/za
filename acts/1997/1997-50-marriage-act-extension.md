# Marriage Act, Extension Act 50 of 1997

ACT

To extend the operation of the Marriage Act, 1961, to the whole of South Africa; and to make provision for matters connected therewith.

(Afrikaans text sigtied by the Pt-esidet?t. ) (Assetlted to 6 November 1997.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, asfollows— Extension of operation of Marriage Act to whole of South Africa 1.( 1 ) The operation of the provisions of the Marriage Act, 1961 (Act No. 25 of 1961), is hereby extended to the whole of South Africa. 5

(2) The provisions of subsection(1) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 27 April 1994.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Marriage Act, Extension Act 1997.
