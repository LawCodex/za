# Public Accountants' and Auditors' Amendment Act 5 of 1997

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existin_g enactments.

ACT

To amend the Public Accountants' and Auditors' Act, 1991 (Act No. 80 of 1991), so as to extend the powers of the Public Accountants'- and Auditors' Board with regard to the prescribing of fees and the imposition of levies; to authorise that board to borrow or raise money with or without security; to authorise the board to recover the costs connected with the review by the board of an accountant's and auditor's practice from that accountant and auditor; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
