# Adjustments Appropriation Act 1 of 1997

ACT.

To appropriate. adjusted amounts of money for the requirements of the State in respeet of the financial year ending 31 March 1997.

(English text signed by the Acting President.) (Assented to 28 February 1997.) B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa. as follows:-

#### 1. Appropriation of adjusted amounts of money for requirements of State

1\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66of1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending 31 March 1997. as a charge to the State Revenue Account; the adjusted amounts ?f money shown in column 1 of the Schedule.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Adjustments Appropriation Act, 1997. Act No. 1, 1997 ADJUSTMENTS APPROPRIATION ACT, 1997
