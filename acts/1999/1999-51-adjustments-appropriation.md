# Adjustments Appropriation Act 51 of 1999

ACT

To appropriate adjusted amounk of money for the requirement of the Stite in respect of the financial year which ended on 31 March 1999.

#### 1. Appropriation of adjusted amounfi of money for requirement of Sbte

1\. (1) Amounts which were authorised in terms of section 7 of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), as a charge against the National Revenue Fund in respect of the financial year which ended on 31 March 1999, are hereby appropriated as shown in the Schedule.
