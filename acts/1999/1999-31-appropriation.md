# Appropriation Act 31 of 1999

ACT

To appropriate amounts of money for the requirements of the State in respect of the financiaI year ending 31 March 2000.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, asfollows:—

#### 1. Appropriation of amounts of money for requirements of Stiate

1\. Subject to the provisions of the Exchequer Act, 1975 (Act No. 66 of 1975), there are hereby appropriated out of the National Revenue Fund for the requirements of the State in respect of the financial year ending31 March 2000, the amounts of money as set out in column 1 of the Schedule.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Appropriation Act, 1999.
