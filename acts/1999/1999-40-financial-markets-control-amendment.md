# Financial Markets Control Amendment Act 40 of 1999

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 30 September 1999.)

ACT

To amend the Financial Markets Control Act, 1989, so as to further regulate the Minister’s power to make regulations; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- Amendment of section 37 of Act 55 of 1989, as amended by section 19 of Act 7 of 1993 and section 26 of Act 55 of 1995

1\. Section 37 of the Financial Markets Control Act, 1989, is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (e) of subsection (1) of the following paragraph:

“(e) the manner in which the amalgamation of financial exchanges, or of a financial and the transfer of the business of a financial exchange or part of its business to another financial exchange or a stock exchange may take place;“. 10
