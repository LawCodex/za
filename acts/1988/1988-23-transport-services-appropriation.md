# Transport Services Appropriation Act 23 of 1988

ACT

To appropriate a further sum of money for the requirements of the South African · Transport Services during the financial year ending on 31 March 1988 and a sum of money for the requirements of the South African Transport Services during the financial year ending on 31 March 1989.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 21 March 1988.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Appropriation of certain sums of money

1\. The following sums of money are hereby appropriated for the requirements of 5 the South African Transport Services:

(1) In respect of the financial year ending on 31 March 1988, amounts not exceeding R202 000 000 for revenue services in addition to the sum of money · appropriated by the Transport Services Appropriation Act, .1987 (Act No. 30 of 1987), as well as an amount of R18 178 000 for the capital programme in respect 10 of expenditure of an exceptional nature for which provision has notbeen made in the aforementioned appropriation act and which will be financed by means of savings on other projects; and (2) in respect of the financial year ending on 31 March 1989, amounts not exceeding R10 848 000 000 for revenue services and R927 000 000 for the 15 capital programme.

#### 2. Utilization of money

2\. (1) The money appropriated by section 1 (1) shall be utiliZed for the purposes set out in Schedules 1 (i) and 2 (i) and more particularly specified in Part 1 of the Estimates of Expenditure (R.P. 14-1988), as submitted to and approved by 20 Parliament.

(2) The money appropriated by section 1 (2) shall be utilized for the purposes set out in Schedules 1 (ii) and 2 (ii) and more particularly specified in Part 2 of the Estimates of Expenditure (R.P . 14:-1988), as submitted 'to and approved by Parliament.

25 Short title

3\. This Act shall be called the Transport Services Appropriation Act, 1988.
