# Promotion of Local Government Affairs Amendment Act 82 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Promotion of Local Government Affairs Act, 1983, so as to further define "local authority"; to provide that members of the Regional Services Council Association be represented In the Council for the Co~ordination of Local Government Affairs and In the action committee of that Council; and to provide for certain administrative work to be performed by officers In the public service; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
