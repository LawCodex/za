# Usury Amendment Act 100 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ l Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions-in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Usury Act, 1968, so as to further regulate the application of the Act in respect of credit transactions and leasing transactions; to extend the particulars to be stated in instruments of debt; to regulate the recovery and disclosure of finance charges where a variable or non-variable finance charge rate is agreed upon at the conclusion of a contract; to provide for the recovery of certain moneys not permitted by section 5 of the Act; to grant to the Minister the power to exempt certain credit transactions and leasing transactions from the application of the Act; and to increase certain penalties; and to provide for matters connected therewith, (English text signed by the State President.)
