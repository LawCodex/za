# Gambling Amendment Act 39 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ l Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Gambling Act, 1965, so as to adjust the fines which may be imposed for offences in terms of this Act.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 24 March 1988.) B EIT ENACfED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 8 of Act 51 of 1965

1\. Section 8 of the Gambling Act, 1965, is hereby amended by the substitution for 5 paragraphs (i) and (ii) of the following paragraphs:

"(i) in the case of an offence referred to in paragraph (a), (c) or (d), to a fine not exceeding [one] two thousand rand or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year Or'to both such fine and such imprisonment;

(ii) in the case of an offence referred to in paragraph (b) or (e), to a fine not 10 exceeding [two hundred] one thous and rand or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding six months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Gambling Amendment Act, 1988.
