# National Supplies Procurement Amendment Act 25 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the National Supplies Procurement Act, 1970, so as to alter the designations of the responsible Minister and Director-General; and to make further provision in connection with the power of the Minister to prohibit the disclosure of certain information; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
