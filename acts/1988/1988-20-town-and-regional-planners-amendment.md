# Town and Regional Planners Amendment Act 20 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ,] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments. ' Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Town and Regional Planners Act, 1984, so as to substitute the definition of "Minister"; and to provide that a professional land surveyor registered as such in terms of the Professional Land Surveyors' and Technical Surveyors' Act, 1984, may in certain circumstances use the title "township planner" and perform work connected therewith; and to provide for incidental matters.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 14 March 1988.)
