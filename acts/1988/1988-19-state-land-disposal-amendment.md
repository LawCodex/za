# State Land Disposal Amendment Act 19 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the State Land Disposal Act, 1961, so as to grant to a member of a Ministers' Council certain powers in respect of certain State land.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 14 March 1988.)
