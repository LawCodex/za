# Foreign States Immunities Amendment Act 5 of 1988

ACT

To amend the Foreign States Immunities Act, 1981, so as to confirm the immunity of foreign states from the jurisdiction of the courts of the Republic; and to remove certain ambiguities; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
