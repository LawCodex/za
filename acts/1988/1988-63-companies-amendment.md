# Companies Amendment Act 63 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE: .

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Companies Act, 1973, so as to provide that a retired judge may also be appointed as chairman of the standing advisory committee on company law; to regulate further the effect of the conversion of close corporations Into companies; to extend the period for which a name may. be registered as a defensive name; to authorize all the members or a company to waive notice or a general meeting at which a special resolution is to be passed; and to extend the powers of liquidators to open banking accounts; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
