# Temporary Removal of Restrictions on Economic Activities Amendment Act 67 of 1988

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in exist ing enactments.·

ACT

To amend the Temporary Removal of Restrictions on Economic Activities Act, 1986, so as to make provision for the duration of the Act until 31 March 1994. (Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 17 June 1988.) B EIT ENACTED by the State President and the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 4 of Act 87 of 1986

1\. Section 4 of the Temporary Removal of Restrictions on Economic Activities 5 Act, 1986, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (1) of the following subsection:

"(1) This Act shall cease to have effect on 31 March [1989] 1994.".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Temporary Removal of Restrictions on Economic 10 Activities Amendment Act, 1988.
