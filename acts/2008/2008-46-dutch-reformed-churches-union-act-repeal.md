# Dutch Reformed Churches Union Act Repeal Act 46 of 2008

ACT

To provide for the repeal of the Dutch Reformed Churches Union Act, 1911; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Repeal of law

1\. The Dutch Reformed Churches Union Act, 1911 (Act No. 23 of 1911), is hereby repealed.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Dutch Reformed Churches Union Act Repeal Act, 2008.
