# The Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Act Repeal Act 47 of 2008

ACT

To provide for the repeal of The Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Act, 1961, and The Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Amendment Act, 1970; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Repeal of laws

1\. The Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Act. 1961 (Act No. 24 of 1961) and the Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Amendment Act, 1970 (Act 4 of 1970), are hereby repealed.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called The Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa (Private) Act Repeal Act, 2008.
