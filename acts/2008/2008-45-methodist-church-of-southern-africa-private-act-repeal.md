# Methodist Church of Southern Africa (Private) Act Repeal Act 45 of 2008

ACT

To provide for the repeal of the Methodist Church of Southern Africa (Private) Act, 1978; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

B EIT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:—

#### 1. Repeal of law

1\. The Methodist Church of Southern Africa (Private) Act, 1978 (Act No. 1ll of 1978), is hereby repealed.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act is called the Methodist Church of Southern Africa (Private) Act Repeal Act, 2008.
