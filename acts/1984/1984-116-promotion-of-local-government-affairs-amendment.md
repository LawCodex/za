# Promotion of Local Government Affairs Amendment Act 116 of 1984

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

. Act No. 116, 1984 Words underlined with solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Promotion of Local Government Affairs Act, 1983, so as to provide that certain powers of the administrators of the provinces shall be exercised only In accordance with the directives of the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning; and to provide for incidental matters.
