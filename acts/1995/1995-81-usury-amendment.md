ACT' To amend the Usury Act, 1968, so as to make provision for the assignment of certain functions and powers to a competent authority within the jurisdiction of the government of a province; and to provide for matters connected therewith. (English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 28 September 1995.) · B EIT.ENACTED by the Parliament of.the·Republic of.South.Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Insertion of section 12A in Act 73 of 1968

1\. The followi~g section is hereby inserted in the Usury Act, 1.968, after section 12: Short title

"Assignment of functions and powers by Minister

12A. The Minister may by notice in the Gazette assign any function assigned to and any power conferred upon the Registrar by or under this Act to a competent authority within the jurisdiction of the government of a province.".

2\. This Act shall be called the. Usury Amendment Act, 1995.
