#### 4. Amendment of section 9 of Act 74 of 1988

4\. Section 9 of the principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (3) of the following subsection:

"(3) The Board may delegate or assign, subject to such conditions as it may deem fit, to any committee established under subsection (1) [or to the Toll Roads Committee], any of its powers, functions or duties, but shall not be divested of any power, function or duty so delegated or assigned and may amend or withdraw any decision made by such committee in the exercise of a power or the performance of a function or duty so delegated or assigned.".
