#### 2. Repeal of section 45quat of Act 13 of 1944

2\. Section 45quat of the principal Act is hereby repealed.

#### 3. Repeal of laws

3\. The laws mentioned in the Schedule are hereby repealed to the extent reflected in the third column of the Schedule .

. Extension of application of Act 13 of 1944

4\. The principal Act shall apply throughout the Republic .

#### 5. Short title and commencement

5\. This Act shall be called the Land Bank Amendment Act. 1995, and shall be deemed to have come into operation on 27 April 1994.
