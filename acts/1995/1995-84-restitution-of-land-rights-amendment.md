# Restitution of Land Rights Amendment Act 84 of 1995

ACT

To amend the Restitution of Land Rights Act, 1994, so as to provide for the designation of an acting President of the Land Claims Court; to provide for the appointment of additional and acting judges of the Land Claims Court; and to further regulate the remuneration and conditions of employment of judges of the Land Claims Court; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
