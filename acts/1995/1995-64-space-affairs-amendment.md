# Space Affairs Amendment Act 64 of 1995

ACT

To amend the Space Affairs Act, 1993, so as to make provision for the appointment of a vice-chairperson to the South African Council for Space Affairs; and to make further provision for the delegation of certain powers and duties; and to delete an obsolete provision; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
