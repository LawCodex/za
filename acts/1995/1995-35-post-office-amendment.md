# Post Office Amendment Act 35 of 1995

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

[ ] Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactwents.

ACT

To amend the Post Office Act, 1958, so as to alter the definition of "Bank"; and to remove the requirement for Telkom SA Ltd and SA Post Office Ltd to obtain State guarantees in respect of all financial instruments issued by them; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
