# Internal Peace Institutions Act Repeal Act 28 of 1995

ACT

To ·repealthe Internal Peace Institutions Act, .1992; and to provide for matters connected therewith.

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 10 July 1995.) B EIT ENACI'ED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa , as follows:-

#### 1. Repeal of Law~

1\. The Internal Peace Institutions Act, 1992 (Act No. 135 of 1992), sections 3, 4 and 5 of the Judicial Matters Amendment Act, 1992 (Act No. 143 of 1992), and the Internal Peace Institutions Amendment Act, 1993 (Act No. 149 of 1993), are hereby repealed.

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Internal Peace Institutions Act Repeal Act, 1995.
