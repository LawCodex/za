# National Building Regulations and Building Standards Amendment Act 49 of 1995

ACT

To amend the National Building Regulations and Building Standards Act, 1977, so as to delete a definition and to insert one; and to empower a local authority to accept the appointment of certain persons registered in terms of the Engineering Profession of South Africa Act, 1990, to carry out certain work; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
