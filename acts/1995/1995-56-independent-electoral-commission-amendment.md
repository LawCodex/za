# Independent Electoral Commission Amendment Act 56 of 1995

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE: .

r. :,_ Words underlined with a solid line indicate insertions in existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Independent Electoral Commission Act, 1993, so as to provide for the filling of a vacancy in. the membership of the Special Electoral Court; and to provide for matters connecte~ therewith.

. . .

(English text signed by the President.) (Assented to 28 September 1995.) BE IT ENiCTED by the Parliament of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

#### 1. Amendment of section 32 of Act 150 of 1993

1\. Section 32 of the Independent Electoral Commission Act, 1993, is hereby amended by the addition of the following subsection:

"(3) A vacancy which occurs in the Special Electoral Court shall be filled by a judge of the Supreme Court designated by the Chief Justice and appointed by the President.".

#### 2. Short title

2\. This Act shall be called the Independent Electoral Commission Amendment Act, 1995.
