# Mines and Works Act 27 of 1956

ACT

To consolidate and amend the laws relating to the operating of mines and works and of machinery used in connection therewith.

(Afrikaans text signed by the Governor-General.) (Assented to 2nd May, 1956.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Union of South Africa, as follows:-
