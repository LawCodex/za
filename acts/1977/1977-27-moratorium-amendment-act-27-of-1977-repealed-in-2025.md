# Moratorium Amendment Act 27 of 1977 [Repealed in 2025]

ACT

To amend the Moratorium Act, 1963, so as to include in the definitiqn 9,f "service" certain service rendered by a citizen allotted in terms of Chapter vm of the Defence Act, 1957, to the South African Police; and to provide for the suspension of the obligation of a citizen rendering service to pay certain debts which become payable while he is rendering service; and to provide for incidental matters.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 16 March 1977.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate"and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:- .

1\. Section l of the Moratorium Act, 1963 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act) is hereby amended by the substitution for the definition of "service" of the following definition:

"'service' means the continuous service in the Citizen Force which is rendered during the periods referred to in section 22 (3), or the continuous service in a commando which is rendered during the periods referred to in section 44 (3) of the Defence Act, 1957, or the continuous service which is rendered in terms of section 34A (10) of the Police Act, 1958 (Act No. 7 of 1958), by a citizen who has been allotted to the Citizen Force, the commandos or the South African Police in terms of Chapter Vill of the Defence Act, 1957, and includes any service on which a citizen allotted to the Citizen Force or the commandos is employed in terms of Chapter X of the latter Act, during the periods of continuous service referred to in sections 22 (3) and 44 0) of the latter Act, and if such citizen contracts any illness or sustains any injury as a result of such service, he shall be deemed to be rendering service during any period during which he is undergoing treatment in hospital for any such illness or injury if such treatment is commenced during the said periods of continuous service.''.
