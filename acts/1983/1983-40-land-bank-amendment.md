# Land Bank Amendment Act 40 of 1983

GENERAL EXPLANATORY NOTE:

E Amendment of section 26 of Act 13 of 1944, as amended by section 9 of Act 47 of 1959 ..

Short title.

l Words in bold type in square brackets indicate omissions from existing enactments.

ACT

To amend the Land Bank Act, 1944, in order to increase the maximum amounts of advances which may be made on se curity of land.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 13 Aprill983.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the ·House of · Assembly of the Republic of S<?uth Africa; as follows:-

1\. Section 26 of the Land Bank Act, 1944, is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (1) of the following subsection:

"(1) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2), no advance made on the sole security of land shall exceed- ( a) [four-fifths of] the fair agricultural or pastoral value of the land, together with the value of improvements to be effected thereon, as determined by the board; or (b) in the case of Crown land which is held under agree- 10 ment of purchase and the full purchase price whereof has not been paid, [four-fifths ofl the amount actually paid in respect of the purchase price, plus [four-fifths of] the value (as determined by the board) of improvements made since the agreement of purchase; or (c) in the case of Crown land held under lease, [four-fifths of] the value (as determined by the board) of the lessee's interest in that land.".

2\. This Act shall be called the Land Bank Amendment Act, 1983. . . 20
