ACT

To amend the Indecent or Obscene Photographic Matter Act, 1967, so as to extend the meaning of the words "cinemato· graph film". · (English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 3 June 1983.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. Section 1 of the Indecent or Obscene Photographic Matter Act, 1967, is hereby amended by the insertion before the defini· tion of "committee" of the following definition: - 5 ·

"'cinematograph film' includes any magnetic tape or other obJect consisting of material of whatever nature, .on which any image or images have bee-n recorded in such a manner that such image or images will be capable o being exhibited as a moving picture or otherwise through any mechanical, electronic or other device;".
