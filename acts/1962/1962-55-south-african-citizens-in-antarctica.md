# South African Citizens in Antarctica Act 55 of 1962

ACT

To apply the laws of the Republic, and to make provision for the administration of justice, with reference to South African citizens in Antarctica.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 21st May, 1962.)
