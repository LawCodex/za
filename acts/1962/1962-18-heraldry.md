# Heraldry Act 18 of 1962

ACT

To make provision for the establishment of a bureau of heraldry and a heraldry council; for the grant, registration and protection of coats or arms, badges and other emblems;

and for other matters incidental thereto; and to amend the Protection of Names, Uniforms and Badges Act, 1935.

(Afrikaans text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 7th March, 1962.)
