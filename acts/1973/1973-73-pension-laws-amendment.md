# Pension Laws Amendment Act 73 of 1973

ACT

To amend section 15 of the Pension Laws Amendment Act, 1968, so as to increase the bonus payable to a person who is in receipt of a military pension; to amend section 4 of the Parliamentary Service and Administrators' Pensions Act, 1971, relating to previous pensionable service; to provide for pension benefits to certain officers in the Public Service in respect of certain periods of employment; to provide for the deduction of certain moneys from a pension or benefit payable to certain pensioners; to provide for the increase of certain pensions and the payment of an allowance oi: bonus to certain pensioners; to repeal certain laws; and to provide for incidental matters.
