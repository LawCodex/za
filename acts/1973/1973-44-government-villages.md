# Government Villages Act 44 of 1973

ACT

To provide for the control and management of Government Villages.

(English text signed by the State President.) (Assented to 21 May, 1973.)

BE IT ENACTED by the State President, the Senate and the House of Assembly of the Republic of South Africa, as follows:-

1\. In this Act, unless the context otherwise indicates-

(i) "Government Villages" means the former milit~ry camps used for residential purposes and situated at Benoni, Germiston, Randfontein, Vereeniging, Cra dock Place (Port Elizabeth), Collondale (East London) and Oribi (Pietermaritzburg); (iii)

2\. (1) The Secretary shall control and manage Governmen t Villages subject to the directions of the Minister.

(2) In the performance of his functions under subsection (1) the Secretary may-

(a) in Government Villages-

(i) maintain, alter, demolish, destroy and reconstruct buildings, structures, fences and other improve ments on land;

(ii) plan, develop and redevelop land;

(iii) construct, reconstruct and maintain roads and streets;

(iv) provide, improve and maintain services;

(v) provide, develop and maintain such amenities, including equipment, as are, in the opinion of the Secretary, in the interests of the inhabitants of Government Villages;

(vi) perform such other work in respect of buildings, structures, fences, improvements on land, land, roads, streets, services· and amenities as is, in the opinion of the Secretary, in the interests of the inhabitants of Government Villages;

(b) (i) allot and let accommodation in Government Villages;

· (ii) in consultation with the Treasury, determine the limits of the income of persons to whom such accommodation may be allotted and let;

(iii) let land, buildings and structures in Government Villages;

(iv) in consultation with the Treasury, fix rentals in respect of such accommodation, land, buildings and structures;

(v) collect such rentals;

(vi) without obtaining a judgement or decree of the 4 No.3907 Act No. 44, 1973 Secretary shall administer certain moneys.

3\. The Secretary shall administer the moneys appropriated by Parliament in respect of Government Villages, and shall for the purposes of the Exchequer and Audit Act, 1956 (Act No. 23 of 1956), be the accounting officer in respect of such moneys.

4\. Any reference in any agreement or · other document to the Board of Management of Government Villages or the chairman of that board or the Controller of Government Villages shall be construed as a reference to the Secretary.

5\. (1) The Government Villages Act, 1950, is hereby repealed.

(2) Anything done by the Controller of Government Villages under any provision of that Act, shall be deemed to have been done by the Secretary under the corresponding provision of this Act.

6\. This Act shall be called the Government Villages Act, 1973.
